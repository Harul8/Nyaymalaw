What it holds

Honest about what it holds

Nyaymalaw answers from the Acts and judgments it holds, for Telangana and the Union of India. Where its law or your facts run out, it says so instead of guessing.

Telangana and the Union of India

What it says when it can’t tell

Not assessed

The date the cheque was presented to the bank was not given, so NM does not say whether that step was in time.

Assumption stated

The 30 days are counted from 10 March, that day left out, ending on 9 April. The agreement’s own terms may count them differently.

Gap named

The material NM holds does not establish which procedural code governs a complaint filed in 2026, so the answer tells you to confirm it before filing.

How held law is shown

The law as held, not as remembered

Statutes, word for word

The passage a statement rests on opens beside the answer exactly as NM holds it, with the words that matter marked and whether the Act is in force.

Judgments, paragraph by paragraph

A judgment is shown at the paragraph relied on, with what the court did with it.

Your papers are your facts

Documents you add are read as your facts and marked as coming from you. They are never treated as the law NM holds.

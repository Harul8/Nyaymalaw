Privacy notice

Version 2026-09-21

Nyaymalaw keeps these details to create your account, sign you in and keep the account secure, and for nothing else:

  • Your email address: your sign-in name, and where password-reset links are sent.
  • Your password: kept only in a scrambled form that cannot be read back.
  • Your sign-ins: when you signed in, an identifier for the browser you used, and the network address of sign-in, registration and password-reset attempts.

You can withdraw this consent at any time, and your account is then closed. You can also ask to see, correct or erase your details, or raise a grievance, by writing to . If it is not resolved, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

AI data sharing notice

Notice dated 15 October 2026

OpenAI processing of your matters

To understand your instructions, research the law and prepare answers, Nyaymalaw sends to OpenAI’s API: your messages and the conversation; the text of documents you add, including the words read from scanned pages; the audio of voice notes you record, to transcribe them; and the legal passages retrieved for your questions. This can include personal and confidential client information. Do not submit material you are not authorised to share, or information subject to a prohibition on disclosure.

This permission is for that purpose only. Photos you keep as evidence, video, embeddings and other providers are not covered; any other use needs a new notice and your permission. You remain responsible for checking client authority and confidentiality obligations.

Processing is global and may occur outside India; India-only processing and zero retention are not promised. OpenAI states that data sent through its API is not used to train its models unless the sending organisation opts in. OpenAI keeps abuse-monitoring logs, which can include what was sent and returned, for up to 30 days, or longer where the law requires. Nyaymalaw sends requests with response storage turned off where the API offers it; this does not remove that retention. Account-specific retention arrangements have not been independently verified.

Giving this permission is your choice and is asked for once, separately from the privacy notice. Without it you can still register and open your matters, but Nyaymalaw cannot research or answer. Withdraw at any time in Profile → AI data sharing; withdrawal stops later requests, including retries, but cannot recall a request already sent or delete what a provider has retained.

This acceptance is not a professional or legal-compliance certification.

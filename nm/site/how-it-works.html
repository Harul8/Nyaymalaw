How it works

From your brief to a checked answer

Here is what you do, what Nyaymalaw does, and what you get back.

What you do

Four steps, one dispute at a time

Nyaymalaw works one dispute at a time, and shows its working.

  1. 1

    Open a matter

    Say who you act for and what you want from NM. Leave out what you do not know yet; it can follow in the conversation.

  2. 2

    Brief it

    Write or dictate the facts in your own words, and add documents, photos or a voice note. Each document is scanned for malware and read before it is used.

  3. 3

    Read the answer

    A few minutes later you have a table at the top and one section for each dispute: what to do, by when, and what to have ready.

  4. 4

    Reply and decide

    Give NM what it asked for, and make the calls it leaves to you. The matter board keeps track of what is in hand and what is not.

What it does not do

Support, not substitution

Nyaymalaw is for practising advocates. It does not give legal advice to clients or to the public.

It does not file or send anything

NM researches and advises. It does not carry out actions for you.

It does not decide for you

Each answer is a draft for your review. You stay responsible for the advice you give.

It does not guess

Where a fact or the law is missing, it names what it would need instead of filling the gap from memory.

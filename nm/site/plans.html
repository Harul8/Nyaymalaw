Do the prices include GST?

No. All prices are per month and exclude GST.

Who is each plan for?

Pro and Elite are each for one advocate, with one account. Enterprise is for chambers and firms.

What separates Pro from Elite?

Usage credits and priority support. The product itself is the same.

Do I pay to try it?

Registration is open, and every new account starts with a trial allowance to try NM.

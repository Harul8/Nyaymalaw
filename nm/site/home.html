How it works

From your brief to a checked answer

Nyaymalaw works one dispute at a time, and shows its working.

  1. 1

    You brief it

    Describe the matter in your own words. Add documents or a voice note. Unknown details can follow.

  2. 2

    It researches

    It finds each dispute and searches the Acts and judgments it holds for the questions that matter to it.

  3. 3

    It writes the answer

    One section per dispute: what to do, by when, what to have ready, and what could still go wrong.

  4. 4

    It checks its own work

    Each section is reviewed against its sources, and the last day of every time limit is counted by code.

You decide. The answer is a draft for your review, and you stay responsible for the advice you give.

What an answer rests on

Open the source, right beside the answer

Every citation opens the passage it relies on, with the words that matter marked. If a statement has no source, it does not make the answer.

Dishonoured cheque

A section 138 complaint appears available if the buyer drew the cheque on an account it maintained for a legally enforceable debt, and the cheque was presented within six months of its date or its validity period, whichever is earlier.

You report that the cheque was dishonoured for insufficient funds, that the notice was sent on 15 September and received on 18 September, and that nothing has been paid. The ordinary complaint last day is Wednesday 4 November 2026.

What it says when it can't tell

Not assessed

The date the cheque was presented to the bank was not given, so NM does not say whether that step was in time.

Assumption stated

The 30 days are counted from 10 March, that day left out, ending on 9 April. The agreement's own terms may count them differently.

Gap named

The material NM holds does not establish which procedural code governs a complaint filed in 2026, so the answer tells you to confirm it before filing.

Questions

What advocates ask first

Not answered here? Write to .

Does it replace an advocate's judgment?

No. Nyaymalaw supports your work. Each answer is a draft for your review, and you remain responsible for the advice you give.

Can I rely on the citations?

Every statement is tied to a passage Nyaymalaw holds, and quotations are checked by code against that passage. You can open the passage beside the answer. Check it, as you would any authority, before you rely on it.

Where does my matter data go?

It is stored sealed in your workspace. If you allow it, your messages, documents and voice notes are sent to OpenAI to produce answers; you can withdraw that permission in Profile at any time.

What law does it cover?

Telangana and Union of India law, as held in Nyaymalaw's own collection of Acts and judgments. Outside that, it says it cannot tell.

What happens when it can't tell?

It says so and names what it would need: a missing fact, a date, or law it does not hold. It does not fill the gap from memory.

What does it cost to start?

Registration is open. The sample answers and plan details are open to read, and every new account starts with a trial allowance to try NM. Plans are priced per month, excluding GST.

Is this legal advice for the public?

No. Nyaymalaw is for practising advocates, and it does not give legal advice to clients or to the public.

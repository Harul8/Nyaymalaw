What we keep about you

Nyaymalaw keeps these details to create your account, sign you in and keep the account secure, and for nothing else:

  • Your email address: your sign-in name, and where password-reset links are sent.
  • Your password: kept only in a scrambled form that cannot be read back.
  • Your sign-ins: when you signed in, an identifier for the browser you used, and the network address of sign-in, registration and password-reset attempts.

What you tell Nyaymalaw about a matter, and the documents you add, belong to your workspace and are used to answer you.

Where your matters are kept

Your account and matters are stored sealed in your workspace. A saved conversation is read back exactly as it was saved; it is never reworked later.

Documents

Every document is scanned for malware before anything reads it. NM tells you which pages it could read and which it could not, so a gap in a document is never silent.

What goes to OpenAI, and only if you allow it

To understand your instructions, research the law and prepare answers, Nyaymalaw sends to OpenAI’s API:

  • your messages and the conversation;
  • the text of documents you add, including the words read from scanned pages;
  • the audio of voice notes you record, to transcribe them; and
  • the legal passages retrieved for your questions.

This can include personal and confidential client information. Giving this permission is your choice, asked for once and separately from the privacy notice. Without it you can still register and open your matters, but Nyaymalaw cannot research or answer. You can withdraw it at any time in your profile; withdrawal stops later requests, but cannot recall a request already sent or delete what a provider has retained.

OpenAI keeps abuse-monitoring logs, which can include what was sent and returned, for up to 30 days, or longer where the law requires. Nyaymalaw asks for response storage to be turned off where the API offers it; this does not remove that retention. Processing is global and may occur outside India; India-only processing and zero retention are not promised. OpenAI states that data sent through its API is not used to train its models unless the sending organisation opts in.

Signing in and sessions

  • A session ends after 30 minutes without activity, and you are warned two minutes before.
  • You can see every device you are signed in on, and end the others.
  • Repeated failed attempts from one address or connection are paused for a short while, and the count ages out. Nothing is locked for good.

Your rights

You can withdraw your consent at any time, and your account is then closed. You can ask to see, correct or erase your details, or raise a grievance, by writing to . If it is not resolved, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

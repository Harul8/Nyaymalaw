What it is

A research and matter workspace for advocates

Nyaymalaw is for advocates practising Telangana and Union of India law. It researches the Acts and judgments it holds, works out each time limit, and checks every section against its sources before you see it. It supports your work; it does not replace your judgment.

Sources beside every statement

Open any citation and read the passage it rests on.

Time limits from the Act’s words

It quotes the Act; code counts the days and states its assumptions.

Plain about what it can’t tell

Missing facts, and law it does not hold, are named, not guessed.

    What still holds

      Every conclusion, and every revision

      The legal premises

      A limitation rests on which provision governs, what starts the period, and which forum binds. Where I inferred one I say so and do not enter a deadline; confirm it and the date becomes a deadline. Correct arithmetic cannot establish any of the three.

      Relief and enforceability

      A legally strong claim can still be worth nothing — the debtor has no assets, the order comes too late, the decree cannot be executed, or the suit costs more than it recovers. I read relief against the objective on availability, value, timing and enforceability; a route that costs more than it wins is flagged, not withheld. State what you know and I will not treat a remedy as delivering when the file says it cannot.

      Briefing readiness

      Answering a turn is not the same as finishing the intake. I am ready for the task only when no controlling gap is open. Where you cannot obtain something, mark it — I pause it with a resume trigger instead of asking again, and say plainly that intake is not complete while it is outstanding.

      Custody, attribution and decisions

      Three questions this file has to be able to answer: which dispute a document belongs to, what has been decided about the advice, and what is being kept or erased and why. None of them is answered by a default — an unattached document contributes nothing until you say where it belongs, and a decision recorded here is not authority to file, send, settle or concede.

      PREPARING, NOT DOING

      Get the document and the hearing ready.

      Everything on this screen is preparation. Nothing here is filed, sent, offered or conceded — you do that, and you record what happened.

      The drafting package

      A package says what is being drafted, for whom, and where every sentence came from. It can be ready to review. It is never ready to file: that is a judgement about a court's requirements which nothing here has checked.

      Filing and sending

      I prepare, verify and export. You file or send it and record the receipt. There is no connector here, and nothing on this screen can reach a court, a client or an opponent.

      Hearing and negotiation

      Preparation is built from the verified package. A witness plan records what to ask about; it never records what a witness will say, and an instruction to an expert states the question rather than the answer.

      Coming back, and handing over

      Where this matter stands, what changed while you were away, and what is still yours. A handover moves responsibility only when the person receiving it accepts.

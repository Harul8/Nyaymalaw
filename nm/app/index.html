Privacy notice

Nyaymalaw keeps these details to create your account, sign you in and keep the account secure, and for nothing else:

  • Your email address: your sign-in name, and where password-reset links are sent.
  • Your password: kept only in a scrambled form that cannot be read back.
  • Your sign-ins: when you signed in, an identifier for the browser you used, and the network address of sign-in, registration and password-reset attempts.

You can withdraw this consent at any time, and your account is then closed. You can also ask to see, correct or erase your details, or raise a grievance, by writing to haaruln@gmail.com. If it is not resolved, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Nyaymalaw is responsible for these details, through Rahul Lambade, Founder & CEO.

You can register, open and access your own matters without accepting this. Research and answers require it. Change your choice in Profile → AI data sharing.

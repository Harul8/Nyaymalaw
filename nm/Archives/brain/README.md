# Served legal brain

`/api/turn` runs `BrainService` and the current `nm/brain` modules. A new chat
starts without an invented matter or board. A source-checked opening can reveal
the board; a greeting or general question can remain a recoverable pending chat.
Every interpreted item, including `answer`, reaches the response writer and
independent reviewer. The interpreter returns routing and evidence decisions;
the checked continuation owns response construction.

This document describes the served source review on 6 October 2026. The six
activity handshakes are active: scoped account coverage, exact research pools,
current source-purpose reuse, typed record results, saved work seals and atomic
release/replay. The [six-activity review](../../docs/backlog/nm-brain-six-review-20261006.md)
records the initial owning functions and captured offline validation at `6bb88d1`.
The [semantic-hardening results](../../docs/backlog/nm-brain-semantic-hardening-results-20261006.md)
record the later production freeze `40c0a52`, exact selected checks and open
semantic defects. The
[standing instructions](../../AGENTS.md) govern future changes; the Before Build
sheet in the [canonical workbook](../../docs/Nyaymalaw_Implementation_Plan.xlsx),
LB-175–LB-197, remains the work queue. Offline contracts verify the exercised
mechanical boundaries. Real-model and browser qualification remain deferred
by the user.

## Six activities and their owners

| Activity | Existing owners | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Attributed account, corrections and omissions | `conversation`, `history`, `record_review`, `disputes`, `material`, their verifiers and state projections | Preserve the complete attributed account; distinguish work authority from account evidence; check record operations and independent account coverage. |
| 2. Substantive and procedural law | `retrieval`, `legal_requirements`, `requirements_state` | Search the supplied corpora for the actual purpose, read exact passages and preserve search/reading limits. |
| 3. Party argument and court treatment | `legal_requirements`, `source_snapshots` | Identify who states each proposition and how the court/source treats it; a reported or rejected submission is not an adopted rule. |
| 4. Apply law to evidence for both sides | `legal_requirements`, `continuation`, `continuation_verification` | Use checked legal passages and attributed account evidence; retain uncertainty and distinguish a source rule, necessary application and limited analogy. |
| 5. Opposing arguments and supported responses | The same research, writer and reviewer owners | Examine relevant contrary support and the response to it without inventing another account or an unsupported conclusion. |
| 6. Justified position and next steps | `continuation`, `continuation_verification`, `work_state`, `execution_contracts`, `turn` | Deliver scoped supported work, preserve pending goals and questions, and distinguish an answer, a checked record outcome and completion. |

These are responsibilities, not six mandatory model stages on every message.
Activities 4–6 share one writer and reviewer; there is no extra personality,
empathy, progress or adversarial classifier.

## Evidence, requested work and actual effects

Each reader receives the complete attributed conversation; exact source IDs
resolve to owned original words. NM formulations remain derived interpretations,
not their own evidence. Retrieved text and conversation content are input, not
instructions. Missing or oversized context is explicit rather than silently
trimmed.

Fresh interpreted items declare `material_purposes` (`account_contribution`,
`interpretation_review`, both or neither) and a `record_requirement` (`none`,
`review` or `change`, with the applicable owned targets, operation and desired
success condition). Code derives whether material reading is required. A
contribution need not invent an edit obligation; an authorised interpretation
repair can use original prior evidence without a new factual assertion.

Before record proposals, a candidate-free source-treatment read classifies the
whole owned advocate catalogue. Readers and Judges reuse it. Work instructions
can authorise reconciliation but cannot supply the underlying account. Exact
source membership does not establish meaning, materiality, truth, actor identity,
legal force or matter ownership; those remain independent semantic judgments.
Dispute and detail Judges also receive the original catalogue and canonical
records for requested coverage, including legitimate empty/no-change reviews.
A current faithful record can already cover the account; no new row is required.

Code records returned stages and canonical before/after owned effects. A planned
purpose, model assertion or successful call is not fulfillment. Prepared receipts
are distinct from committed effects. `execution_contracts` checks typed outcomes
against the exact request, owned effects/current state, independent review and
current or inherited goal scope in the served composition and release path. The
writer declares `performed`, `already_current`, `review_no_change`, `unresolved`
or `none`; the response Judge independently checks the relevant meaning. Even a
partial reply cannot positively fulfill a requested review while its whole
independent account coverage remains partial or unassessed. An unresolved result
can retain actual checked narrower effects without closing the wider goal.
Selected evidence meaning and whether the outcome satisfies the desired condition
still require the independent reviewer; mechanical validation does not prove
semantic accuracy.

Code appends the actual record entries added, revised, withdrawn or retained,
and a visible unfinished-reading notice when applicable. This display comes from
canonical state changes, not the model's assertion. It is bound to the receipt
and released response; an ordinary no-change reply need not cause a mutation.

`work_state` projects tasks and questions from released units, with server-owned
IDs and explicit transitions. Fresh record requirements, independent result checks,
record snapshots and result seals travel with the saved work. A promise does
not deliver a document or answer a question. Untracked history stays untracked. A diversion preserves existing work;
a narrow answer does not silently complete a wider or inherited review goal.
Scoped completion does not close a matter or authorise external action.

## Research and source readback

Research subjects own their purpose, scope, question and relevant attributed
record. General legal enquiries need no invented matter or dispute. Act and
judgment retrieval uses local hybrid search and reranking. Reader proposals and
independent source checks distinguish principles, conditions, supporting/adverse
reasoning and gathering items. Applicability, court/date/jurisdiction metadata,
source treatment and search coverage remain separate.

An empty reading is not evidence that no applicable law exists. The empty-reading
Judge checks the exact supplied passage pool, including useful conditional or
adverse support; a genuinely empty supplied pool is a code-owned fact requiring
no Judge call. A nonempty finding's selected citations do not establish coverage
of the full retrieved pool. The served turn now supplies the exact search pool
to the existing nonempty Judge and conditional empty Judge; a selected subset
cannot silently stand in for that pool. All absence and sufficiency judgments
are bounded to the supplied passages, not complete legal discovery, currentness
or binding authority. A single supported
proposition may span exact owned contiguous fragments; meaningful long content
is not rejected merely for exceeding a former excerpt/owner/condition width.
Foreign, reversed or unowned ranges still fail their source proof.

`response.research_reads` is the canonical research record; the board is a
projection. Reuse needs matching purpose, scope, question, record, corpus revision
and verification contract, plus coherent explicit checking evidence and no unread
work. Current source-purpose dependencies must also match the exact original
references used by saved application premises. The served turn passes its latest
complete canonical source-purpose catalogue to that check. New unrelated messages
do not invalidate those original premises; missing old/current checking evidence
keeps history readable but nonreusable. Historical sources remain readable without
invented upgrades. Current legal composition requires the current support contract; corpus freshness alone
does not renew old verification. Loaded indexes refuse reads if their artifacts
have changed until reloaded.

Released blocks retain authoritative source snapshots. Authenticated readback
checks ownership, identity, digest and the released block, and reopens the saved
passage independently of later corpus availability. Inline links are code-derived
from checked references; selected legal support must be reachable. Speaker,
proposition and treatment labels do not create additional legal authority.

Every fresh block selects a closed evidence expression, including accounts,
assessments, questions, follow-ups and completion. Code renders complete owned
original words, checked legal propositions/passages and fixed status templates.
Writer-authored display text and review/seal fields cannot become expressions.
Completion and record acknowledgements remain code-owned regardless of links.
Rendering fidelity does not establish correct semantic support or legal use.

## Calls and bounded recovery

Normal generative calls vary with the work actually due:

| Stage | Normal calls | Conditional work |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Interpret | 1 | One contract correction; opening repair is separately conditional. |
| Advocate-source treatment | 1 when material/opening is read | One existing contract correction. |
| Dispute extraction and check | 1 + 1 for requested material review | Scoped coverage can require checking even with no proposed dispute. |
| Detail extraction and detail/opening check | 1 + 1 for requested material review | Scoped coverage can require checking even with no proposed detail. |
| Query planning | 1 per context-fitting due batch | Reused research adds no planning call. |
| Passage reading | 1 per context-fitting batch with supplied passages | No passages skips this model stage. |
| Nonempty finding/source check | 1 per context-fitting batch | The existing call independently checks the exact full supplied pool. |
| Empty-reading check | 1 conditional batch when an empty reading had supplied passages | No supplied passages adds zero; a mixed batch may need both nonempty and empty checks. |
| Response writer and independent reviewer | 1 + 1 | Every interpreted item participates, including a greeting. |
| Search/reranking, projections, receipt checks and atomic save | 0 generative calls | Vector/embedding infrastructure is separate from this count. |

A normal greeting or read-only continuation is **3 calls**. A material turn with
both proposal/check stages and response composition is **8 calls**. A requested
empty material review also reaches both independent record Judges; no proposals
alone cannot establish success. A due nonempty research batch adds
**3**, giving **11** when combined with material. An empty reading over supplied
passages uses its conditional Judge; a mixed empty/nonempty batch may add one
more Judge call. A first general legal question with useful findings normally
costs **6**. Successful exact replay costs **0**.

These are normal paths, not fixed ceilings. Context-fitting batches, malformed
contracts, output truncation and conditional opening/response recovery can add
calls. Each reader/verifier has its existing one-correction bound; checked peers
are retained when the owner actually receives valid verdicts. A strict adapter
can hide all siblings in a schema-invalid envelope; code cannot retain decisions
it never receives. No per-candidate retry cascade or restarted local review loop
is introduced. Unread opening `O1` must not restart an exhausted review.

The one turn-owned recovery ledger allows eight conditional reservations and
reserves two for reply generation/review corrections; upstream repair cannot
consume that reserve. Recovery does not reset the ledger in nested owners.

A rejected response can receive one replacement generation and another independent
check; retained subsets must satisfy their own checks. Actual `metrics.llm_calls`
and content-free `model_calls` receipts report dispatches, tiers, latency and
tokens; `provider_retries` counts transport attempts separately. Configured Judge
role and actual returned tier are checked. Separate calls using one model can
still have correlated errors; role separation is not model diversity.

## Release and failure boundaries

One atomic commit saves accepted input, admitted records, exact released response,
research, coverage and progress. An exact durable replay returns that response
without duplicate effects or model calls. Authentication and core ownership are
checked before save. An unconfirmed save cannot support a saved-message or
performed-success fallback; a lost acknowledgement needs exact durable confirmation.

The single authoritative matrix is `nm/shared/gates_contracts.py`: `G-CORE` and
`G-COMMIT` withhold the turn for core evidence/save integrity; `G-EFFECT` withholds
only an unsupported positive result; `G-INCOMPLETE` discloses incomplete independent
review and refuses full review completion while preserving checked effects.
`G-MODEL` retains its existing per-need scope. The fresh rows are consulted by the
served owners and are marked built with owning checks and six current generated
exports. Registry presence alone is not enforcement proof.

Genuine semantic rejection, dependency withholding and unread review are distinct.
A required unread restoration successor cannot retire the prior merged account;
independent checked peers survive. Optional empty metadata, whitespace and exact
repeated valid selectors are normalized only where meaning is unchanged. They do
not acquire a new blocking gate. Every consequential refusal needs a precise owned
mismatch. Gate counts measure friction, not a false-rejection rate.

Legacy missing goals/results remain untracked. Exact inherited review aliases
require the same retained requirement and owned scope; the server does not guess
equivalence from paraphrases. Replay checks the original saved prefix rather than
requiring an old result still to be current after a later correction. Historical
absence is not new independent omission/absence proof.

Offline fixtures and failure injection check routing, bounds, state/effect binding,
peer preservation and persistence contracts. Real-model accuracy, omission detection,
false acceptance/rejection, latency and unfamiliar-conversation/browser quality
remain deferred by the user. The flow does not execute filings, send communications,
export a final document or close a matter.

# 03.03 — Assess disputes and possibilities

Link supplied case material to retrieved law, analyse causes, elements, proof, adverse facts and possible theories, construct source-backed requirements and consider opposition across the file.

This is a responsibility within the legal-brain loop, not a mandatory execution
stage or a claim of complete feature acceptance. Files inside this folder stay flat.

## Boundaries

Reasoning does not establish factual truth, authenticity or credibility. The source-backed checklist and work annotations retain their native interpretation and merge owners. Facts, corrections and recorded advocate answers remain owned by Work the file. Child-only finish_opposition remains confined to its existing child scope.

Architectural roles and dependency directions remain explicit in
[`nm/source_layout.json`](../../../source_layout.json). No new tool permission,
provider-processing approval or release claim comes from moving a file.

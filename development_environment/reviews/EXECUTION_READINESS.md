1. **Build and completion dependencies are different.** Packets consume named
   scoped outputs. Full-item prerequisites are explicit. Item completion still
   requires all final criterion owners and registered dependencies. The combined
   graph is checked, including cycles across the two kinds of node.
2. **Foundation work no longer depends on its eventual consumer being finished.**
   BK-79 sits with briefing integration; BK-88 explicitly retains actual
   confidential-path processing, custody, deletion and restoration proof at W2.
   Pilot/production profiles include it. No stronger obligation was discarded.
3. **The data/API plan now reaches actual transfers and failure states.** It
   specifies request/response shapes, errors, idempotency, version checks,
   reauthentication, recovery generations, upload chunks, protected downloads,
   jobs, migration/cutover and unknown external-delivery outcomes.
4. **Test design cannot masquerade as execution.** Each packet owes every linked
   criterion's requirement, planted control and required evidence method. Shared
   scenarios are additional baseline anchors, not a blanket 175-criterion pass.
5. **Human choices have explicit stop points.** Recommendations are usable for
   synthetic foundation work; procurement, real-model/paid runs, new coverage,
   confidential processing and external action retain their own approval gates.
6. **The workbook is an audited view.** Exact populations, status projections,
   formulas and authored source hashes are reconciled. Text hashes normalise
   line endings. Captured execution results are labelled historical snapshots,
   avoiding a result that changes the test that certified it.

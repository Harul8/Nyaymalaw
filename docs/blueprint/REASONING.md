# Reasoning principles

Owner-authorised refinement, 22 September 2026. Before Build owns requirements;
this is their technical behaviour view, not a certification of conformance.
Runtime guidance lives in core/conversation.py; existing domain controls retain
ownership of permissions, calculation, provenance and release. The paired
[communication principles](COMMUNICATION.md) govern presentation.

| ID | Required behaviour and forbidden outcome | Verification obligation |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Ground material case assertions in authorised records and legal propositions in retrieved sources or reviewed rule data. Keep allegations, inferences and established facts distinct. Source presence is not semantic support or applicability. | Source, quote and attribution checks plus semantic evaluation. G-BINDING is need-scoped, not a whole-turn prohibition. |
| R2 | The model interprets and proposes; code enforces mechanical boundaries. An independent-step decision retains its exact step, input identity and concise basis. Unknown is not independent. | Malformed/unavailable/changed-step tests, private audit persistence, and measured unsafe false-clear and safe false-block rates separately. A schema-valid verdict is not proof of judgment. |
| R3 | Assess thresholds material to the requested work: jurisdiction, procedure, standing, limitation and relevant prerequisites. Do not force a filed role onto advice-only work. | Each threshold needs its own coverage; one limitation gate does not prove them all. Missing premises restrict dependent advice, not safe evidence gathering. |
| R4 | Calculate from attributed events, governing dates and reviewed rules. Preserve conditional alternatives and whose claim is assessed. Arithmetic cannot establish applicability. | Premise and calendar boundary tests, supported factors, changed inputs and opposing claims. Do not turn a conditional calculation into a definitive deadline. |
| R5 | Separate candidate discovery from canonical identity. Approximate search may rank but cannot silently identify an Act or merge parties/cases. | Exact identity, alias, version and locator checks. Search scores are not probabilities of legal correctness. |
| R6 | Research consequential uncertainty and credible adverse propositions with permitted sources. Record coverage and negative results. Stop on sufficient support, non-progress, budget or a required decision. | Research execution and non-progress tests plus semantic review. A negative search does not establish that contrary law does not exist. |
| R7 | Keep positive, negative and unassessed results distinguishable; add stale, partial, disputed and unavailable states where meaningful. | Malformed-state refusal and visible failure-path tests. Not every object must have exactly three states. |
| R8 | Reopen work dependent on changed facts, instructions, law or permissions; preserve history and explain material revisions. | Selective invalidation, persistence and served-history tests. Do not silently reuse stale advice or invalidate unrelated issues. |
| R9 | Keep extraction, understanding, factual support, legal support, applicability, practical uncertainty and readiness distinct. Each controlling unknown has a consequence and resolving evidence. | No aggregate confidence may clear another dimension. Persistent state and semantic evaluation are separate obligations; guidance alone is not a full uncertainty model. |
| R10 | Compare plausible hypotheses before choosing a revisable preferred position. Preserve material alternatives and evidence that would distinguish them; put the strongest supported opposing case. | One stored preferred theory is not a ban on alternatives. Never assert incompatible facts simultaneously; judicial possibilities remain conditional. Reassess when material changes, not only once per file. |
| R11 | Generalise the causal mechanism, not the incident's wording. Retain genuine, reviewed jurisdiction- and statute-specific rules. | Meaning-preserving variations and distinguishing boundaries. Do not replace enforceable controls with a purpose statement or narrative interpretation with phrase branches. |
| R12 | Compose owned guidance once without conflicting local instructions, preserving schemas and untrusted-data boundaries. | Structural assembly checks plus semantic review and behavioural probes. A prompt scan cannot certify that all possible contradictions are absent. |
| R13 | Match advice maturity to support. Give a considered recommendation only when its material premises permit it; otherwise conditional help, explanation or a question. | Maturity/refusal controls and professional evaluation. User insistence does not create evidence, permission or certainty. |

## Evidence discipline

Record mechanism, exercised population, exact version, actual result and remaining
limitation separately. A named test is not a passing run, a suite label is not an
executable suite, and an evaluator that could notice a defect has not observed it.
Never report a count of principles as completeness.

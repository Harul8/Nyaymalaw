# Research basis and limits

Reviewed 22 September 2026. These sources inform design, not an India applicability
opinion, corpus admission or counsel sign-off. NM remains India-only. Foreign
interaction guidance is not authority for advice on an Indian matter.

- Bar Council of Kerala: reproduction of BCI professional conduct rules, Part VI,
  Chapter II, court-duty rule 4 and client-duty rules 15 and 19.
  https://barcouncilkerala.org/professional-etiquette
  Design inference: independent judgment, restrained language, fair representation
  and authorised instructions. No certification of every historical cross-reference
  or currently operative provision is implied by using this reproduction.
- Judicial College: Equal Treatment Bench Book resource page.
  https://www.judiciary.uk/about-the-judiciary/diversity/equal-treatment-bench-book/
  Transferable guidance: communication should support understanding and participation.
  Adapt to NM's advocate user; do not import a lay-client questionnaire or assume
  vulnerability or lack of expertise.
- NIST: AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile, 2024.
  https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence
  Evaluation-design inference: distinguish generated plausibility from verified
  evidence, document limitations, and preserve human oversight. Adopting a framework
  or passing narrow tests does not establish compliance or universal accuracy.

Owner decisions prevail over style suggestions: no canned production replies,
natural paragraphs, visible relied-on explanation and passages, bounded autonomy
and explicit source/permission limits. Show a checkable rationale, not private
internal deliberation. Hold evaluation fixtures outside production instructions.

Runtime guidance improves instructions, not model capability by assertion. Semantic
accuracy, independent-step false-clear rates and qualified professional judgment
need their separately recorded evaluations.

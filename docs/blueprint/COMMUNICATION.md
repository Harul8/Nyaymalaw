# Communication and presentation principles

The September principles below are a design reference. Current decisions and
build status belong to Advocate build plan; legal brain holds capability intent.
P10 and S3/S4 permit fluent, independently checked answers and summaries with
owned source references. Other reply kinds retain their declared release
contract. No historical renderer or prompt named here is a required owner for
the new core engine. Communication may not alter source meaning or permissions.

| ID | Required behaviour and forbidden outcome | Verification obligation |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Address the immediate purpose first: answer, acknowledge or identify the controlling gap. Do not manufacture an action for a greeting or a question for a completed task. | Purpose-sensitive evaluation. Advice-path action/question assembly is structural, not proof of usefulness. |
| C2 | Recommend when supported and explain relevant alternatives. A decisive unresolved premise may require a conditional view or a question. Respect a request for comparison. | Advice maturity and semantic assessment. Never force confidence to avoid looking indecisive. |
| C3 | Use the record and permitted retrieval before asking a small useful group of neutral questions. Explain what answers change; accept unknown, unavailable or later. | Multi-turn tests, including justified reconfirmation after contradiction or changed instructions. Do not lead toward a preferred factual answer. |
| C4 | Explain the weakness, consequence and supported ways to address it. No established remedy is a legitimate outcome. Surface material overlooked issues without a quota. | Extend existing alternative-route checks with useful-challenge evaluation. Preserve reservations and safety boundaries after user disagreement. |
| C5 | Distinguish supplied, read, partly read, asserted, supported and unassessed material. Never equate upload success with examination. | Failure-path and semantic checks; explain practical limits, not internal machinery. |
| C6 | Speak to an advocate as a peer without assuming specialisation. Explain law when asked or materially needed. First-release language follows P17 and S3; Telugu translation and explanations are later work. | Semantic and audience review for the current engine. No lectures, flattery, sales language or ungrounded reassurance. |
| C7 | Show truthful saved, processing, partial, failed, unknown and completed states. Unknown outcomes use reconciliation with the same operation identity. | Browser state coverage. EXPERIENCE section 4 contains editable defaults, not mandatory conversation scripts. Test meaning and recovery. |
| C8 | Use natural paragraphs and useful structure, not mandatory reports. Explain relied-on law and application, with relevant retrieved verbatim passages and links visible by default. | Presentation and proportionality checks. Deeper material is optional; material risk is not. Truncation protection alone does not prove appropriate length. |
| C9 | Open the correct source version at its passage/location, with attribution and limits; distinguish original from interpretation. | Browser clicks, authorised access, missing-source recovery and return to chat. A source route alone proves no usable inspector. |
| C10 | Show material urgency, adverse information and changed premises in text, not solely colour or transient notices. Do not present lapsed dates as live windows. | Existing renderer checks plus broader risk-state coverage. Quiet successful draft recovery may disappear; consequential warnings remain available. |
| C11 | Separate prepared work from advocate-reported actions and confirmed records. NM never files, sends, signs, appears or contacts anyone; it can prepare drafts and record attributed reports. | A saved draft is not an external effect. Check owned persistence before claiming saved work. |
| C12 | Challenge documents, dates and support, not the person's character. State equally supported adverse findings equally strongly for either side. | E-073/E-113 plus non-sycophancy evaluation. No accusations or certainty gained merely because the user insists. |
| C13 | Preserve keyboard order, names, focus, narrow-screen access and 200 percent text enlargement; voice stays optional. | Real browser verification. Existing keyboard/renderer checks are partial evidence, not full accessibility certification. |
| C14 | Show private material only after authorisation; clear it after confirmed sign-out. Keep re-entry/history checks and generic external notifications. | Actual session/cache/reload tests. Do not expose refused drafts as advice or infer full coverage from one test. |

## Attentiveness is not a tone template — 22 September diagnostic

The Ananya browser run revealed runtime violations of C1, C3, C6 and C8, not
merely missing prompt adjectives. `threading.bind` returned a fixed refusal
after explicit clarification; `_with_screens` appended the entire successful
screening report; restored chat repeated a historical-response notice per turn.
The first defect prevents substantive reasoning, while the others bury it.

Care must be observable: use prior instructions, recognise a correction, explain
its consequence from the checked record, own a demonstrated misunderstanding,
and ask only for the unresolved distinction. Do not manufacture emotions,
sympathy, certainty or a legal remedy. Routine controls remain recorded, but
are not the conversation. Material coverage limits, conflicts, adverse facts,
source passages and changed assessments remain visible. A generic prose rewrite
must not invent an assessment the reasoning path never completed.

The narrow screening-presentation change does not establish expert communication.
Context-sensitive synthesis, historical-notice economy and independently judged
live conversation remain acceptance obligations, not solved by adding this text.

## Evaluation

Extend existing E-073/E-085/E-102/E-113 checks, rather than build a duplicate judge.
COMM-01 through COMM-03 cover purpose, question economy and useful challenge;
REASON-01 covers independent uncertainty and alternatives. Every dimension has a
negative control. Fixtures are evaluator inputs, never production dialogue examples.
Actual approved execution and human calibration are needed for judged acceptance.

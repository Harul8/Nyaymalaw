# D53

Written out from the Advocate build plan by plan_export.py; the sheet is the record (P7).

## From [AN.39](../rows/AN.39.md), Description

D53. One reading view for law wherever its link is (the answer, the board, a point's pane): the Act or the judgment by name, the section or paragraph, only the relevant passage, the words used marked where they are saved; reading the whole Act or judgment is an option (owner: "only the paragraph with reading the whole doc act or judgement as optional"). The whole is read from the held law by the passage's own identity, never by a name or a search, and said plainly when it cannot be read or when the held law has changed since the answer - never something else in its place. A page of the advocate's papers keeps "Show the whole document".

## From [P11](../rows/P11.md), the whole row

Item:
Each advocate owns their own data

Description:
An advocate's conversations, matters and uploads are visible only to that advocate, and only they can export or delete them. Sharing with juniors or colleagues is not offered yet; each uses their own account. Deletion on request removes the advocate's uploads, conversations and everything Nyaymalaw derived from them; anything that cannot be removed at once (backups until they expire, a model provider's temporary retention, records the law requires) is listed and told to the advocate.

Governs or depends on:
P2; 6.4.4, 6.5.5; Q13 for the deletion details

Status:
Ownership and deletion direction decided P11/P19; not implemented by this cleanup

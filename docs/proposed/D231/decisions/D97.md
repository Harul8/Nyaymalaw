## From [AN.45](../rows/AN.45.md), Description

D97. THE LIMIT IS STILL REAL (the owner asked to be told). The search model reads 512 tokens. Cut on their printed paragraphs, 12% of 9,901 paragraphs (800 judgments) are longer, holding 40% of all the words: a vector of a whole long paragraph would not see them. So a paragraph longer than 500 tokens (counted by the model's own tokenizer) is split at sentence ends into the fewest parts of about equal size, each within the limit, every part keeping the paragraph's printed number ("12", part 2 of 3). A paragraph within the limit is one passage.

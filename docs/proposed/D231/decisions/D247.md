THE RECORD OF THE LAST SAVE HOLDS A HASH OF WHAT WAS SAVED, NOT OF THE SEALED FILE. Sealing is not deterministic: the same matter sealed twice gives different bytes, and a file sealed again with its matter unchanged - a record from before sealing, sealed on its next save, or a matter given a new data key - would otherwise read as another file at the recorded version and be refused. What was saved is what the record vouches for. A hash of a whole conversation, with its own identifiers and times, confirms nothing to anyone who does not already hold it.

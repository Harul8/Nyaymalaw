# D74

Written out from the Advocate build plan by plan_export.py; the sheet is the record (P7).

## From [AN.42](../rows/AN.42.md), Description

D74. Send is a round button with an up-arrow. Pressed, it becomes a round button with a turning ring and a stop square in its middle (Claude's reading of "radial button", not changed by the owner). The ring turns without a percentage, since how far an answer has got is not known in advance; the status line says what NM is doing. Pressing it stops the answer (D78).

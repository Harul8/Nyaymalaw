## From [AN.53](../rows/AN.53.md), Description

D246 (D241's closed account, REFINED BEFORE BUILD, 15 Nov 2026, under the owner's word to go with Claude's recommendations). AN ACCOUNT IS CLOSED BY AN ADMIN, IN TWO STEPS, AND WHAT GOES IS SAID. Closing cannot be undone, so: (1) WHO: an Admin, not one view-only, with the password confirmed recently, as every change in the Admin area needs (D116, D121); never an admin's account (it is made an advocate first, or taken out of the settings), never one's own. (2) TWO STEPS: the account is suspended first - which is undone by Reinstate, ends its sign-ins and stops new work - and only a suspended account can be closed, and none while NM is working on one of its conversations. The Admin types the account's address to confirm, and the reason must say whose written request it is. (3) WHAT GOES, in this order, each step safe to repeat: every conversation of the advocate's - kept or deleted - as Erase now erases it, its own key first; then every paper in their space, as the papers of an erased conversation go (D244); then their sign-ins with their files, their draft keys, any reset link of theirs still waiting, and last the account record itself. A closure cut off part-way leaves the account there, suspended, and is made again. (4) WHAT IS KEPT, said at the closure: the usage ledger, under the account's digest only, as a billing record (eight years); the content-free counts and timings of turns (two years); turn attempts and step records until their own periods (90 days); the two sign-in logs' lines (one year); and the Admin audit chain, which keeps that the account was closed, by whom, when and why - with the account's address as the change's target - and is never shortened. (5) BACKUPS, said too: a backup made before the closure still holds it, so it is gone from every copy within 35 days.

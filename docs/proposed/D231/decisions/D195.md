# D195

Written out from the Advocate build plan by plan_export.py; the sheet is the record (P7).

## From [AN.51](../rows/AN.51.md), Description

D195-D206 are Claude's proposals from the review, recorded at the owner's instruction; for the owner to correct before each piece is built. A finding marked CONFIRMED was read by two readers or shown by a script; PLAUSIBLE was read and not proven end to end (column 7). Three of them (D200, D201, D202) are the owner's own earlier decisions, brought back for the owner to confirm or change, and nothing is built on them until the owner says.

## From [AN.51](../rows/AN.51.md), Description

D195 (F-02, High). A WRITTEN YEAR THAT NO HELD ACT CARRIES RESOLVES NOTHING. One resolver for 'which held Act is this name'. A name written without a year still proposes every held Act of that title. A name written with a year no held Act carries may still rank the search, but a section is never read by its number from the Act of another year: it is recorded as not held, with code's reason that an Act of the same title and another year is held. The judgments' resolver already answers this way; the two become one rule. Touches AN.47 (D129-D131).

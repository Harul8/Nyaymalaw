## From [AN.51](../rows/AN.51.md), Description

D290 (17 Nov 2026; D202 decided as Claude recommended - Claude had made none, and makes this one). THE CASE ACCOUNT GOES THROUGH THE INDEPENDENT REVIEW, ONCE, AND WHAT IT FAULTS IS LEFT OUT. After the account of the case is made and checked (the gathered account, wired by D291), one review on the review model (6.1 Terra, D289) reads the whole account against the same material - the papers' points, the held law offered for the dispute, the advocate's messages - and names what is wrong: a requirement its law does not set, a status the papers do not bear out, an ask for what the papers already show, a point of the other side or a deciding item that misstates the matter. Code leaves out each item the review faults, and each requirement whose law is not found in a held passage, and says so in its own words - in the line on the account (D185) and, item by item with the review's reason, in the research note. Nothing is rewritten after the review, so nothing needs reviewing again; 'checked' is kept for an account the independent review passed (F-03). Cost: one Terra call a dispute, about $0.05-0.08, to be measured. The board's and the pane's structure is unchanged (D60): the account shows fewer items, and code's line says why.

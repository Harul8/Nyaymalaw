# D245

Written out from the Advocate build plan by plan_export.py; the sheet is the record (P7).

## From [AN.53](../rows/AN.53.md), Description

D245 (D241, REFINED AT BUILD, 14 Nov 2026, at the owner's word of that day - "Don't stop until everything pending is built tested and pushed" - and the standing word to go with Claude's recommendations). A SIGN-IN IS KEPT 90 DAYS AFTER IT ENDS, AND EACH ACCOUNT'S LATEST IS KEPT WHATEVER ITS AGE. Read from the code: a sign-in's record holds the account, the device, a label of the browser and the address it came from, with files of its own for when it was last used and whose it is; an ended sign-in is kept - ENDED, NOT DELETED - so that a sign-out can be told from a forged token; and the Admin area's 'last sign-in' and 'last active' are read from these records. SO: (1) a sign-in whose end is 90 days or more past goes, with its files of use and owner; its end is taken as its expiry, which no sign-in outlives, signed out early or not, so none goes sooner than D241 says. (2) EACH ACCOUNT'S MOST RECENT SIGN-IN IS KEPT whatever its age: without it the Admin area would show an account that has signed in as one that never has. (3) A sign-in whose record cannot be read is kept: its end and its owner cannot be known. (4) The sign-in log's lines about sign-ins are another record, kept one year (D241). The directory's sweep of what has expired (53h4b1) does it, daily.

# D31

Written out from the Advocate build plan by plan_export.py; the sheet is the record (P7).

## From [AN.35](../rows/AN.35.md), Description

D31 WHAT MAKES THE CASE STRONGER (B), replacing the checklist: for each dispute, what the law requires - the elements the governing sections and the chosen judgments' tests set, and who must prove each - each checked against the points read from the papers (shown, with the page; weak or disputed, with why; missing); what to get from the client for what is weak or missing (a document, a record, a certified copy, a witness), why the law needs it and how it helps; and what the other side will rely on and what would answer it. It sits on the matter board under each dispute; the answer's questions to the advocate come from it.

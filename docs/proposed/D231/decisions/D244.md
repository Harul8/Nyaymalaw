## From [AN.53](../rows/AN.53.md), Description

D244 (D241, REFINED AT BUILD, 14 Nov 2026, under the owner's word to go with Claude's recommendations). A CONVERSATION'S PAPERS ARE ERASED WITH IT; A PAPER MORE THAN ONE CONVERSATION NAMES GOES WITH THE LAST OF THEM. Read from the code: a paper is kept in the advocate's own document space - its original as received, in sealed parts, and each record of the text read from it (the first reading, and a new record each time pages are read again) - sealed under the space's one key, which all the advocate's papers share. A conversation keeps in its own record the pages of each paper sent in it, as they were read, and names the paper. Nothing stops two conversations naming one paper: the server takes any reference to the advocate's own. SO: (1) WHAT GOES: every paper the erased conversation names - sent with a message, added while NM worked, or held by a turn not yet finished - its original and every record of its text. (2) WHEN: only after the conversation itself is erased, by Erase now or by the sweep 30 days after Delete; never while it can still be put back. (3) A PAPER ANOTHER OF THE ADVOCATE'S CONVERSATIONS NAMES - one in use, or one deleted and not yet erased - IS KEPT, and goes when the last conversation naming it is erased. (4) NOTHING ON A GUESS: where a conversation that may be the advocate's cannot be read, whether it names the paper cannot be known, so the papers are kept; where the erased conversation could not be read one last time, what it named cannot be known either. Papers kept so, which no conversation names, are D241's papers never sent with a message (30 days), and the retention sweep erases them by the same test. (5) THE KEY: the space's key is shared, so it is not destroyed; a paper's files are removed, its original first and its text last - so that an erasure cut off part-way is still found by the paper's text record and erased again - and a file that could not be removed is named in what the erasure returns. A backup keeps its copy until the backup itself goes, within 35 days (D241). (6) ONLY THE ADVOCATE'S OWN SPACE: an erased conversation's owner is the one the store records - the advocate who asked, or the owner its deletion mark names - never what its record merely says. THE ORDER TO BUILD, one piece each: the originals' delete; the text records' delete; the document desk's erasure of what an erased conversation named; the store's last look at a conversation it erases, and which of an advocate's conversations name a paper; Erase now; the sweep.

## From [AN.45](../rows/AN.45.md), Description

D102. COURT (D92). Every Andhra Pradesh High Court judgment is marked Telangana's by the one rule the statutes' titles already use (citation_contracts STATE_NAME and FORUM): the store's court reads "High Court of Telangana" and the court's own name stays as court_as_filed. The case index's scope statement and the research's judgment lists read the same rule, so "No Telangana High Court judgments are held" is no longer said of a library that holds 4,280 (all dated 2018 or before). The two research prompts that describe the library say "the High Court of Telangana (the High Court of Andhra Pradesh before the State was divided)".

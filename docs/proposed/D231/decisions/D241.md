## From [AN.53](../rows/AN.53.md), Description

D241 (D223). DELETION AND RETENTION. This is a recommendation of common practice for a professional's records, not legal advice; each period marked (confirm) is for the owner's counsel or accountant to confirm. WHAT AN ADVOCATE MAY DELETE: any conversation of their own, with everything that belongs to it - its papers as sent, the text read from them, its line in the history, its turn records and its ratings. NM NEVER DELETES A CONVERSATION BY ITSELF: none expires. HOW: Delete asks the advocate to confirm, naming the conversation; it leaves the history at once; for 30 days it can be put back, because for an advocate an accidental deletion of a client's matter is the likelier harm than a late erasure; after 30 days it is erased - its own key is destroyed first, so that whatever remains of it anywhere cannot be read, then its files are removed. An advocate may ask for erasure at once, told that it cannot be undone. BACKUPS: a backup made before the erasure still holds the conversation and its key; backups are kept at most 35 days (to be said in the hosting guide), so an erased conversation is out of every copy within 65 days, and the advocate is told so at Delete. AN ACCOUNT: closed by an Admin at the advocate's written request; its conversations are erased the same way; the Admin audit chain keeps that it was done. WHAT NM KEEPS, AND FOR HOW LONG: the outgoing-data log, one year (D169, as built); the two sign-in logs, one year, then their older lines are removed (confirm: taken as the same kind of record); a sign-in that has ended or expired, 90 days; a turn's attempt record, 90 days after the turn finished or stopped (it exists so that a repeated send returns the saved answer, which the conversation itself holds); a pending registration or a reset link, 30 days after it expires; a paper uploaded and never sent with a message, 30 days; the counts and timings of turns, which hold no content, two years; the usage ledger - what each account used and was allowed - eight years as a billing record (confirm); the Admin audit chain, kept and never shortened; ratings and turn records, with their conversation. THE COUNT OF STORED PAPERS, which D235 left unset for want of deletion: 2,000 for one advocate, told before it is reached, set once deletion is built. THE ORDER TO BUILD, one piece each: the store's one delete (the key first); the mark, Put back and the 30-day sweep; the route and the page's Delete; then each record's retention with its sweep. NONE IS BUILT.

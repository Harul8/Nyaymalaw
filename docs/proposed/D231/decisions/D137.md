## From [AN.48](../rows/AN.48.md), Description

D137. A CORRECTION REPLACES NM'S READING EVERYWHERE. When the advocate corrects what NM read from a paper (a date, a survey number, a party), the correction replaces NM's reading in every later step, marked 'as you told us', and only the work that depended on the corrected reading is redone. (Claude's detail, for the owner to correct: NM's original reading stays in the saved record, and a saved answer stays as saved, P46.)

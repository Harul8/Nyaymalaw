## From [P26](../rows/P26.md), the whole row

Item:
Answer dispute by dispute

Description:
The whole matter is understood once (facts, parties, timeline, disputes and how they connect). Then each dispute runs its own loop at the same time as the others - research, write, review, rewrite up to the third try, drop what still fails - with the shared facts and a line about each other dispute. Each dispute's section is shown, with its sources in the reading pane and its checklist on the board, as soon as it passes, the most urgent (nearest time limit) first. The overview is written last and reviewed against the sections shown, with a cross-check for conflicts between them. It writes the opening's Do-first line (what to do first, and why) and, where it matters, how the disputes affect each other; where each dispute stands is that dispute's row in 'In short' (P40, 24 Oct 2026). A single-dispute question or a short follow-up stays one section.

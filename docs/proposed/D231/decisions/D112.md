## From [AN.45](../rows/AN.45.md), Description

D112. THE WHOLE JUDGMENT MARKS BY WORDS (extends D100). The passage an answer read is marked in the whole judgment wherever its words stand: the same words, the shorter inside the longer, or a run of twelve words they share (a shorter of fewer than three words never matches by itself). It replaces the test by identity for judgments only, since every passage of a re-cut judgment has a new identity; Acts are marked as before. Words that are not there are still said not to be in the judgment as held today.

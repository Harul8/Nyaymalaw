# D240

Written out from the Advocate build plan by plan_export.py; the sheet is the record (P7).

## From [AN.53](../rows/AN.53.md), Description

D240 (D218's question). THE PER-ACCOUNT SIGN-IN LIMIT IS KEPT. Five wrong passwords for one address pause that address for at most fifteen minutes; nothing is locked and the count ages out. The harm of keeping it: someone who knows an advocate's email can hold that pause in place by guessing. The harm of removing it: one account's password can be guessed from many addresses with no limit at all, and what is behind it is client files. For a legal application the second is the worse. Nothing is built.

(4) The answer: the Act named is the one read for that name - the pick is offered its sections alone; the judgment named is read as a cited judgment held once. 'Let NM judge' leaves it as now (every reading offered; a judgment held more than once not read). An answer after the default stood changes the writing as D44; research already made is not made again (AN.37, remaining item 2). (5) Saved: the question and its answer with the turn, as now; the research keeps, for each such name, the Acts or judgments it may be and which was read (research v19); earlier records read as before.

# D144

Written out from the Advocate build plan by plan_export.py; the sheet is the record (P7).

## From [AN.48](../rows/AN.48.md), Description

D144. DISPUTE BY DISPUTE, RESEARCH ON DEMAND. After the understanding, each dispute is researched only when the advocate asks for it, naming the dispute. After a dispute is researched the advocate may go on to another dispute, add information the current dispute needs, or ask for its analysis. A switch where research and writing begin chooses which disputes run; when it chooses all of them (end to end) the path is exactly today's.

## From [AN.35](../rows/AN.35.md), Description

D32 SPEC (C). Sees: under each dispute, the sections and judgment passages relied on, each element with its status and page, and the evidence to gather. Must never: show an element as proven without the page's words; ask the client for what the papers already show; present a judgment as good law where its standing is not known; read 'not held in our library' as 'no such law'. When it fails: the question, search or judgment not read is said and the rest stands. Passing: on the owner's 8-paper matter, every section the papers cite is fetched, every picked judgment is read, and each dispute has its list of elements tied to pages and an evidence list the owner judges useful.
ORDER (agreed): A2 and A1, then B, then A3; each tried in small runs; applied only at the owner's word.

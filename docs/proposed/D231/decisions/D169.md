## From [AN.53](../rows/AN.53.md), Description

D169 makes the log the kept record of what NM sends out; client material sent with no record of it is the one thing that record exists to prevent, and a record that fails open is the usual way such a record comes to have gaps. So while a line cannot be written, a dispatch to a recipient outside this computer - the AI service, a mail provider, a hosted search - is refused before it is made, and goes again once the log can be written. What stays on this computer (the store, the local index, the local outbox, a stand-in model: the receivers the egress policy lists as in-process) goes on, its lost lines counted as 53e built: refusing it would stop sign-in and saving for a fault in a log. WHAT THE ADVOCATE IS TOLD: a service notice in code's words, with what NM had finished kept and Continue offered - never a server error. That needs its own piece first, because it was found that ANY dispatch the installation refuses part-way through a message is a server error today (measured, 13 Nov 2026). THE COST, said: a full disk or a wrong permission on the log's folder stops every answer until an operator puts it right; the Admin diagnostics already show the lost lines, and readiness can be made to show it.

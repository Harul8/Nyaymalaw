## From [P46](../rows/P46.md), the whole row

Item:
Reopening shows the answer as saved; it is never rebuilt

Description:
A conversation reopened from history shows each answer exactly as it was saved, at once, including answers saved under earlier versions. Reading a saved conversation - opening it, continuing it with a new message, the board, the files, the reading pane and the research note - never rebuilds, re-checks or re-renders a saved answer. What is checked when a record is read is the record itself: the matter file's seal, the chain of digests that links each turn to the one before, and that it belongs to the advocate (conversation.checked_rows). An answer is checked once, when it is made: what is saved is exactly what passed (turn.commit). A record that fails those checks is never shown: the conversation says it cannot be read reliably and its records stay unchanged, as today. A saved record is never changed, and an earlier answer is never presented as fresh advice. New answers still carry their versions, so that the page, the reading pane and the research note know how to draw them. Overrides P35's 'show a saved answer that does not replay exactly under this code'; P35's integrity check stays.

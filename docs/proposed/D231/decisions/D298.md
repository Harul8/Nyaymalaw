## From [AN.48](../rows/AN.48.md), Column 10

D298. After a go-ahead (Research all, Write, Done) the answer pane shows only "Preparing a response…" for the whole step - no stage, time or steps - and a question the step asks would not open; reopening the conversation shows them. Cause found in the page: the event stream's first message is the previous step's "stopped", which the page reads as the stream's last word, so it closes the stream and nothing asks again. Proposed: the page's watch of a step it has just started ends only on that step's own finish or stop. A defect against 48a and 48f5; nothing on the board or the reading pane changes.

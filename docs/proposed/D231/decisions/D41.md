## From [AN.37](../rows/AN.37.md), Description

D41 (agreed): COST IS AN INTERNAL MEASURE, never a question to the advocate. Every model call's cost is estimated before it is sent and kept beside what it cost; a turn's cost is estimated roughly at its start and again once its plan is known; a counter keeps what it has spent against that estimate. The measure is the owner's, against spiralling cost (what NM does when the counter passes the estimate: open, see Status).

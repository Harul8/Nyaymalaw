## From [AN.33](../rows/AN.33.md), Description

D23 THE ADVOCATE CHOOSES THE MODEL AND THE EFFORT. Beside the send box: 6 Luna (the default), 6.1 Terra, 6.1 Sol, each said as a cost beside Luna's ("about 8-10x", "about 16-20x"); effort Low, Medium (the default), High, Max, which tunes the reasoning of every step. The chosen model is used for every step of the turn; the choice resets to Luna and Medium for each new conversation; each saved turn records the model and the effort it ran with, and reopening never runs it again.

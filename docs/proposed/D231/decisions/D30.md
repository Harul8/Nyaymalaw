## From [AN.35](../rows/AN.35.md), Description

D30 COMPLETE AND RELEVANT RESEARCH (A). A1: the law the papers, the advocate's messages or the understanding name - a section of an Act, a judgment the papers cite - is looked up by name in the held law, with the sections its Act links to it; what is named and not held is said. A2: no fixed count on the relevant law that is kept and read. A3: once per dispute, the law still missing is named and searched for once.

## From [AN.35](../rows/AN.35.md), Description

D30 RELEVANCE (the owner's clarification): relevance gates every step and nothing spirals - (i) the model reads each candidate and only what it picks is read; (ii) what it is shown stays ranked by relevance, a page of the most relevant for each question from each library (twenty sections, twenty judgment paragraphs): the breadth of what is looked at, never a cut of what is kept; (iii) named law is fetched once with its own linked sections, never links of links; (iv) the missing-law round runs once per dispute and searches only for what the model names as missing; (v) a reading never starts a search; (vi) every run counts what was offered, picked and read for each question, so a spiral would show.

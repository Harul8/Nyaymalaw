# P41

Written out from the Advocate build plan by plan_export.py; the sheet is the record (P7).

## From [P41](../rows/P41.md), the whole row

Item:
Each dispute's section in the same order, its detail folded

Description:
Each dispute's section opens with a heading that says what it is and what to do (for example 'Cheque of Rs 4,80,000: complaint under section 138'), then, in this order and each only where it has something to say: the advice and its date, with the deciding reasons on the advocate's facts; where (the forum, where one is needed); what could still go wrong (the conditions and missing facts that would change the advice); next steps, numbered. The caveat that the facts are as the advocate reported them is said once, under the heading. Reasoning that does not change the advice (for example proof, interest, an alternative remedy) is shown under a folded 'More on this' at the end of the section: the writer marks which paragraphs those are, and code folds them. Nothing is cut: a folded paragraph is reviewed, linked and saved like any other. A paragraph the advice depends on is never folded: the check is shown which paragraphs are folded and says if one should not be.

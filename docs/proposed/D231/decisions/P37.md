## From [P37](../rows/P37.md), the whole row

Item:
A passage opens in the reading pane at once

Description:
A link in an answer opens its passage in the reading pane at once, as the answer on screen holds it: the same saved passage, read with the answer from the conversation the server checked when it was opened. The pane then asks the server, as before, which confirms the passage is the one saved and gives NM's saved checks for it; meanwhile the pane says it is confirming the passage and its checks, and the checks read 'being read', never 'not assessed'. Checks the page holds itself are never shown in their place. If the server's passage does not match, or cannot be read, the words are taken away and the pane says so, as before. When the checks arrive, what the advocate has opened in the pane (the whole section or document, How NM checked this) and where they have scrolled stay as they were. This replaces the 16-17 Oct rule that the pane shows nothing until the server's read returns. Also fixed: for every answer saved dispute by dispute (turn v6 and later, from 19 Oct 2026) the pane said 'Source checks: Not assessed - saved check evidence is unavailable' and never flagged a check that did not come out clean, because the page knew turn versions v1-v5 only; it now reads the saved checks of any core turn, the server deciding which turn versions it holds.

Governs or depends on:
P35; P31; nm/app/brain-sources.js

# D161

Written out from the Advocate build plan by plan_export.py; the sheet is the record (P7).

## From [AN.48](../rows/AN.48.md), Description

D161. ONLY CRITICAL INFORMATION COSTS AT ONCE. The message reader (the model) judges how critical new information is and what it affects. Only critical information causes work straight away: the analyses it affects are written again, and what is new is researched where it needs research (D156). Information that is not critical is kept as a message (D159) and read by every step still to come, with nothing redone at once. A correction always shows on the board at once (D148); the analyses resting on it are written again at once only when it is critical. Claude's detail, for the owner to correct: a correction to a date or a figure that a worked-out time limit uses is always treated as critical, by code, and the time limits are worked out again.

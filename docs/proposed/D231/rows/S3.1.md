# S3.1 - Language

Row 59 of the 'Advocate build plan' sheet, written out by plan_export.py. The sheet is the record (P7).

## ID

S3.1

## Item

Language

## Description

Plain professional English addressed to an advocate: precise, direct and proportionate to the request. Use the advocate's facts with their attribution and uncertainty, and the source's legal meaning and conditions. Express an obligation only when the checked law supports it, without requiring a literal word such as must. Attribute holdings only where the deciding court adopted that proposition in its reasoning, with qualifications preserved. Explain unsettled or unassessed points. Never guarantee an outcome.

## Notes

FINDINGS, measured 5 Nov 2026.
JUDGMENTS (1,015,780 passages from 33,790 judgments; Supreme Court 87%, Andhra Pradesh High Court 13%; no Telangana High Court). (J1) A paragraph's stored number is a count of the pieces it was cut into, not the judgment's own: of the passages that open with a printed paragraph number, 71% carry a different stored number - so 'paragraph 12' in an answer or the reading pane is often not the judgment's paragraph 12. (J2) Long paragraphs were cut into fixed-length pieces (32% of passages); 69% of those pieces begin mid-sentence, and 27% of all passages begin, 31% end, mid-sentence. (J3) What a passage says of itself is thin: its citation 20%, the sections it cites 5%, the cases it cites 1%; 27% of paragraphs are of an unknown kind. (J4) 1.5% of passages repeat another's words exactly (1,741 judgments). (J5) Length suits the model: median 210 tokens, 0.3% past the 512 it reads.
ACTS (351,182 passages, 1,621 Acts). (A1) Every passage opens with a citation header (e.g. the Act's name with its jurisdiction, year and number, the section and its title) that is 37% of its characters at the median and 68% at p90; the header decides its vector: two divisions of one section are 0.92 alike in the index (0.89 encoded again) but 0.60 on their own words, against 0.80 for two sections of one Act and 0.57 for two Acts - the vector search finds the section by its name more than by what a clause says. (A2) 13% of section heads (each carrying its whole section) are past the 512 tokens the model reads, so their vectors miss the rest; their divisions are searched on their own. (A3) The section heads hold their divisions' words again (83 and 65 million characters). (A4) 7.5% of passages repeat another Act's words (state versions, amending Acts); the one-copy register hides 1.5%. (A5) 64 Acts are held as blocks of text, no section found; 242 have no standing recorded.
THE SEARCH. Exact search over every vector (no approximation): one query 0.05-0.16 s on the Acts and 0.15-0.63 s on the judgments - not a cost worth an approximate index.
THE FOLDER. 30 GB, of which about 20 GB the served search does not read: the v3 and v4 stores and their backups, the older word indexes as JSON, the summary indexes, and today's .before copies (2.2 GB); chunks.db also holds the old store's 416,571 Act passages, unread.

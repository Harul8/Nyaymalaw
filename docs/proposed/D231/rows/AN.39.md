DECISIONS (agreed 4 Nov 2026):
D52. A point on the matter board opens only that point in the reading pane, as an item did on 31 October: what it is, its mark and who must prove it, why it is needed with the law that requires it (which opens as its passage), where it stands (the points of the papers that bear on it, each page opening in the pane), what to get for it (what, from whom, why, how it helps), and - for a point not yet shown or shown only weakly - "Tell NM about this" and "Not available", each putting a start in the message box. A point the papers show has no buttons (owner: OK). The dispute's name still opens the whole dispute.
D53. One reading view for law wherever its link is (the answer, the board, a point's pane): the Act or the judgment by name, the section or paragraph, only the relevant passage, the words used marked where they are saved; reading the whole Act or judgment is an option (owner: "only the paragraph with reading the whole doc act or judgement as optional"). The whole is read from the held law by the passage's own identity, never by a name or a search, and said plainly when it cannot be read or when the held law has changed since the answer - never something else in its place. A page of the advocate's papers keeps "Show the whole document".
D54. No separate view of a judgment "as NM read it": the pane shows the relevant passage; the complete document is the option (owner).
D55. The answer's links open the same views: each line under "What I need from you" opens its point; the pages of a point open in the pane.
D56. What the other side will rely on is a short group on the board, each opening alone in the pane with what answers it and "Tell NM what answers it".
D57. The board is clean: short phrases, the detail in the pane when clicked. A dispute's points are named in a few words - for new answers the case step gives each point and each thing the other side relies on a short name for the board in the same call (no extra call); answers saved before keep their own words, drawn to two lines with the whole in the pane. The board's other parts (timeline, figures, papers read side by side, what was set aside, how NM understood the matter) become one line each that opens its detail in the pane.
D58. The case step's law quotations are checked after taking off quotation marks wrapped round them, for new answers; an answer saved before stays as saved (P46).
D59. Marking the exact words behind each link of the answer needs the writer's words saved with each link: recorded as a later option, not built.
D60. Once built, the UI and structure of the matter board and of the reading pane are FROZEN: no change to either without the owner's explicit approval.

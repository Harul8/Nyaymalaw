## Notes

PIECE 1 BUILT, 20 Oct 2026 (offline checks with scripted models only; no live run yet).
What a turn now does (turn v6): the matter is understood and researched once; then each dispute's section is written, checked by code and reviewed on its own, side by side (writer v12, review v9; nm/core_engine/answer_sections.py owns the loop). A section gets its draft and up to two rewrites, whatever asks for them; each rewrite the review asks for is told plainly to rest every statement on the advocate's messages, the documents and the held sources, and is shown the words each finding concerns. What the review or the date check still faults after that is left out statement by statement and the rest reviewed again as trimmed, at most twice; a note at the end of the section says how many paragraphs, or which checklist items, were left out and what was found. Where nothing of a section survives, a note built by code alone stands in its place (G-FALLBACK): why; the facts in the advocate's own words; the provisions found for the dispute in their own words; and what NM's working understanding says is still open. With several disputes, the sections shown are checked against each other in one judge call; a section in conflict is corrected once, reviewed, and used only if it passes, under an 'Edited' label with the original kept under 'See original' (G-EDITED); a correction that changes the advice is also said at the top. The overview is written last from the sections shown and reviewed beside them; if it does not pass, it is left out and said so. Sections, and the board's disputes, are ordered most urgent first (earliest worked-out last day). Progress shows two new stages: checking the sections against each other, and writing the overview.
Choices made in building, for the owner's review: (a) what the review finds missing earns the section's rewrites like any other finding; after the tries it is said beside the section, never dropped or withheld. (b) If a rewrite asked for a missing point brings a fault of its own, the earlier draft that passed is kept, with its note. (c) G-GROUND, G-ATTRIB and G-QUOTE now withhold the statement, not the turn (one owner for each rule, so no separate statement gate); code's own exact-source mismatch (a held provision differing from its store readback) still stops the turn, now under G-CORE, whose condition it matches. (d) A turn the AI service fails for in every section is not saved, so the same message can be sent again; a single failing section gets the note. (e) The check across the sections, the corrections and the overview run one after another; to be timed and revisited in piece 2. (f) A turn may make 32 corrections in all, held durably (the shared steps one, each section two, each correction after the cross-check one); a retried turn keeps its count.
Replay: every saved section, trim, correction, cross-check and overview is bound again to its exact evidence on reopening, and what code left out or put in a section's place is re-derived from the saved reviews; saved v1-v5 turns replay unchanged under their own renderings (v1-v16 kept). The earlier whole-answer preparer was retired from production; tests build saved v5 turns with tests/core_whole_answer.py.

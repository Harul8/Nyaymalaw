## Description

THE OWNER'S WORDS, 4 Nov 2026: "also, replace SEND button with an icon, like an up arrow , once it is clicked, instead of greying out, make it a radial button and that sholud also be an option stop the conversation in the middle -- however, during the same time, if new message is typed or files are attached, show send button again and once sent create a queue of these follow up messages - a one liner at the bottom, just above chat text area -- also put an an option to steer(send it, and ongoing work should continue this follow up message and respond accordingly), edit(bring it back to chat area to edit), discard(close the message)"

THE OWNER'S WORDS, 4 Nov 2026: on stopping while words are in the box: "can't stop without clearing them"; on queued messages not steered: "once, but if they are not related, NM can  process them separately"; on building the button, the ring and the queue line first, and Stop and Steer after AN.41: "ok".

DECISIONS (agreed 4 Nov 2026):
D74. Send is a round button with an up-arrow. Pressed, it becomes a round button with a turning ring and a stop square in its middle (Claude's reading of "radial button", not changed by the owner). The ring turns without a percentage, since how far an answer has got is not known in advance; the status line says what NM is doing. Pressing it stops the answer (D78).
D75. The same ring while a message waits for its files to be read (AN.40): pressing it means do not send, and the words and files stay. This replaces AN.40's greyed "Sends when read..." with Cancel beside it (D61 changed so; the rest of D61-D64 stands).
D76. While an answer is worked on, words in the box or a file attached bring the arrow back in place of the ring. There is no stop while the box holds words: the advocate clears them to stop (owner). Pressing the arrow puts the message in the queue, and the ring returns.
D77. The queue: one line per message just above the message box (no longer under the progress in the conversation), each with Steer, Edit and Discard. Edit puts the message back in the box, above anything typed there, with its files; Discard removes it. A message whose files are still being read says so on its line and waits for them before it can be steered or sent (AN.40). The queue is saved with the conversation and survives a reload (Claude's proposal, not changed by the owner: no work is ever lost). A queued message never goes in another conversation (AN.40 D64).
D78. Stop: the answer stops after the step in progress (an AI call already made is paid for); every piece finished is kept (AN.41 D67) and the red message with Continue and Discard follows (AN.41 D69, D71). If Stop cannot reach the server, the ring keeps turning and a line says the answer could not be stopped.
D79. Steer: the message goes into the answer being worked on. NM reads it against its understanding of the matter and decides what it changes - the disputes, the law needed, the papers where files come with it - and only those steps are done again, the rest kept (AN.41 D67, D70); a section already shown is replaced once its rewrite passes. The steer is saved with the answer and the independent review checks the answer against it, so a steer is never silently ignored. A steer that reaches the server after the answer has finished goes as the next message, and the page says so.
D80. Queued messages not steered go once, together, as the next message when the answer is done (as today, 15 Oct 2026); where they are not related, NM processes them separately (owner). How (Claude's proposal): NM's own understanding of that message decides whether they belong together - no rule in code - and answers unrelated ones each on its own, in its own part of the answer.
ORDER (owner: "ok"): D74-D77 - the button, the ring and the queue line with Edit and Discard - need nothing else and may be built early; D78 (Stop) and D79 (Steer) after AN.41, whose saved steps they rely on.
MUST NEVER: a Stop losing finished work; a steer silently ignored; a queued message sent in another conversation, sent before its files are read, or lost on a reload.
PASSING (the owner's browser acceptance): the arrow sends and becomes the ring; words typed bring the arrow back, and pressing it queues a line above the box; Steer, Edit and Discard each do what they say; Stop keeps the finished pieces and shows the red message; a steered answer visibly takes the steer in; a reload keeps the queue.

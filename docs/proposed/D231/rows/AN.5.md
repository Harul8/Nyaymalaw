## Description

SEES: a dispute's section starts being written as soon as its own legal questions are searched and its own provisions and judgment paragraphs picked, without waiting for the other disputes' searches and picks; the first section shows sooner. MUST NEVER: let one dispute's research or failure change another's section; show a section written from research that cannot be replayed exactly; save a stopped turn as if a question it never searched had been searched. WHEN IT FAILS: a dispute whose search or picks fail keeps its own gaps, as today; a turn stopped part way saves what it showed, a question never searched recorded as such. DONE: offline, a dispute's section is written from its own research alone and replays exactly; older turns replay unchanged; a live run measures the first section's time.

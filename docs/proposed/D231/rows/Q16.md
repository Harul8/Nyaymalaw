The time-limit step's dating of the notice period

## Description

Should the time-limit dating error be taken up now? In two of three live runs on 19 Oct 2026 the time-limit step took the bank's return date (3 September) as the date the payee received the bank's information, from which s.138 proviso (b) of the Negotiable Instruments Act runs the thirty days for the demand notice. In one run its own correction removed the date; in the other it kept it, the answer gave 3 October as the last day, the review caught it and the cheque section was held back. Proposal: the time-limit step's check confirms that the words it quotes date the starting event itself, not a related one; where they do not, the date stays not given and that limit is not worked out.

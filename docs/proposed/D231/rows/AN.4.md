## Item

Build: law read connected (P32)

## Description

SEES: each provision whole, in the pick's view and in what the writer reads; with it the provisions the Act itself links to it, and those beside it, each saying why it is there. MUST NEVER: state a rule without its proviso, exception or explanation; follow a reference into the wrong Act or an ambiguous provision; show an unbound reference as law; fill a gap from model memory; drop a link silently. WHEN IT FAILS: a linked section that is not held, or cannot be read, leaves the provision standing and the gap recorded; nothing is substituted; searches and answers saved before this replay unchanged. DONE: the offline checks of P32 pass and one bounded paid live run, with the owner's approval, shows the families read.

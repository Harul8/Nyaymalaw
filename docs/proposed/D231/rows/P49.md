# P49 - A limit is worked out where the facts date the event its status follows from, on a stated assumption

## Item

A limit is worked out where the facts date the event its status follows from, on a stated assumption

## Description

In the time-limit step, where the Act's event is a status or consequence that follows at once from an event the advocate or a document does date (the Act's words say 'when the decree becomes enforceable', the facts date when it was passed), and nothing they say suggests it came later, the step takes the dated event as the Act's event: it quotes the dated words, says under same_event that it is, states under assumptions that it takes the two to coincide, and says under missing what would show otherwise; the answer and the Working box then say so, as they do for P34's period of credit. An event that follows by its own nature at another time (receipt after posting; information reaching a person after a cheque is returned) is not such a case: there, as before, the step gives the later event's date or leaves it empty and says what date is needed. Where nothing dates the event or one it follows from, the limit stays not worked out. Prompt only: no new contract, no new call; code's checks of the words and of same_event are unchanged. AMENDED 25 Oct 2026 after the step-alone checks (v2): the same_event flag is defined once, for every case - true if the quoted words date the very event the Act's words name; also true where the Act's event is a status or consequence that follows at once from the event they date and nothing suggests it came later (on the stated assumption); true, with a period the facts set (P34), if they date the event that period runs from; false only if they date another event whose own date is still to be given.

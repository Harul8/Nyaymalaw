## Item

An advocate is told how much usage is left by notices at 50%, 25% and from 10% down, always as a percentage

## Description

RULE (owner, O22 item 1; the figure is always a percentage of the allowance left, O21): a notice sits above the message box, announced to screen readers, with a button to close it. (1) At 50% left, once. (2) At 25% left, once. Each of these two closes by itself after 10 minutes, or sooner when closed (the owner's words for the second were 'once for 10 mins and then disappear'; the same 10 minutes is read for the first - TO CONFIRM). (3) From 10% left, after every answer that lowers the figure by another 1% (10%, 9%, 8% ... 1%), and it stays until the advocate closes it; closing one does not stop the next. At nothing left the limit screen of P51 replaces them. A notice appears only once an answer is saved, because that is when usage changes. The same steps apply on a paid plan, naming the plan. The top-bar chip changes with the steps (amber at 25% or less, stronger with a warning mark at 10% or less - Claude's proposal, TO CONFIRM). The notice offers See plans (Upgrade from 10% down); it never shows cents, credits or rupees (O21). Dictation shows no price (O22 item 2), and Usage and plan carries no 'How usage works' text (O22 item 3).

# P4 - Keep a dates record, never the official diary

Row 18 of the 'Advocate build plan' sheet, written out by plan_export.py. The sheet is the record (P7).

## ID

P4

## Item

Keep a dates record, never the official diary

## Description

Nyaymalaw keeps a record of the dates an advocate gives it (hearings, deadlines, limitation), each with its source and basis, and shows them. It is never the advocate's official court diary, and says so wherever the dates are shown.

## Governs or depends on

Governs 3.1.4, 6.5.1 and 6.5.3. Reminders and notifications are not decided.

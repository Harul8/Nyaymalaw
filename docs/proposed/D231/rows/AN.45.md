D97. THE LIMIT IS STILL REAL (the owner asked to be told). The search model reads 512 tokens. Cut on their printed paragraphs, 12% of 9,901 paragraphs (800 judgments) are longer, holding 40% of all the words: a vector of a whole long paragraph would not see them. So a paragraph longer than 500 tokens (counted by the model's own tokenizer) is split at sentence ends into the fewest parts of about equal size, each within the limit, every part keeping the paragraph's printed number ("12", part 2 of 3). A paragraph within the limit is one passage.
D98. THE CUT. A judgment is read from its raw text; none of its words is removed or changed. (1) A paragraph begins at a line that starts with a number and a full stop or bracket; the judgment's paragraphs are the longest rising run of such numbers (one may be missing; a numbered list inside a paragraph, starting again at 1, is not a paragraph). A judgment is cut on printed numbers only where the run reaches five paragraphs and three quarters of its range; otherwise it is cut as unnumbered. (2) What stands before the first printed number - the court's heading, the bench, the headnote - is the judgment's opening, cut by size. (3) A judgment printing no paragraph numbers (37% of those sampled; mostly the older Supreme Court reports) is cut by size at sentence ends into passages of about 300 tokens, each "passage N" - NM's own order, never offered as the court's. (4) A paragraph number is only ever the court's own. (5) Sentence ends are told by code that does not take Mr., No., Sec., v., Rs., an initial or a decimal for one; a stretch with none inside the limit is cut at a line end, and last at the limit.
D99. WHAT EACH PASSAGE CARRIES. The old fields (case, name, court, year, cites and cited-by counts, source file; keywords left empty, nothing reads them) and: paragraph_num (the printed number or none), part and parts, section (opening, paragraph or text), passage_no, locator (what an answer and the pane show: "12", "12 (part 2 of 3)", "opening", "passage 7"), court and court_as_filed (D102), citation (D103), cited_cases and sections_cited (D104), paragraph_type and the table column atom_type (D105). The words are exactly the raw text's; the vector and the word index are made from them.
D100. THE SERVED CODE (built with the store, applied with it, on the owner's word).

# P3 - Citations are the moat

P3

## Item

Citations are the moat

## Description

Every care is taken with citations. Every case and statute citation Nyaymalaw shows is resolved by exact key against the held corpus and read back before it is displayed; quotations are verbatim. A citation the corpus does not hold is reported as not held in this corpus, never as non-existent or wrong; one that could not be checked is shown as not assessed. A 'verify my citations' service checks the citations in an advocate's own question or draft (A7, B5).

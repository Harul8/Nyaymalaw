## Description

The reranker reads each passage whole. Today's reranker (bge-reranker-v2-m3) is given its own long window, capped at about 8,000 tokens for a question and its passage together, instead of 512: the question with its search phrases and nearly every held passage are read in one go, and a shorter pair uses only its own length. Measured 20 Oct 2026: every judgment paragraph is under 3,300 characters, and all but 35 of the 353,689 Act passages are under 32,000 characters (about 8,000 tokens); those 35 - the longest the Income-tax Act's s.10 at 173,000 characters - are still read in pieces within the cap, never cut off. Pairs are batched by length so that short ones are not padded to long ones and long ones do not overflow the graphics card. Each batch is as large as the point where the graphics card's speed stops improving, measured on the machine it runs on, and never above a memory ceiling that leaves room for anything else on the card (about 6.5 to 7 GB of this laptop's 8 GB); a batch that still runs out of memory is split and tried again.

The mechanical check that a fact's quoted words are in the papers (research.code_checks) matches against the pages joined as they are, so a sentence that runs over a page break, with the page's stamp, signature and the reader's marks between the halves, is 'not found'. The same matching is written three times (research.py, matter_board.py, response_rendering.py) and its message does not say which words failed.

(8) NOT TOLD. The loop's last 'revise' is handed to the writer and the review as data (planning_issues) but nothing makes either say that the law was not researched or that NM's own check was not satisfied.
(9) A FIELD MEANS TWO THINGS. The understanding's 'ruled_out' is shown on the board as the advocate's standing instructions, but the model fills it with what the papers do not establish.

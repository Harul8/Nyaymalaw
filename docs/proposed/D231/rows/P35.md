## Item

Reopening a saved conversation is near-instant

## Description

SEES: the conversation history, a reopened conversation, its board, its files and anything opened in the reading pane appear at once, however long the answers saved in it. MUST NEVER: rebuild a saved answer to show it (P46, 24 Oct 2026: it is shown exactly as saved); skip the integrity check of what is read from disk; let one advocate's listing read another's conversations beyond what ownership needs. WHEN IT FAILS: as today, a conversation that cannot be read reliably says so and its records stay unchanged. DONE: each derivation inside a saved turn's replay is made once (not once per check that needs it); a saved turn, identified by its chained digest, is replayed once per server process; a repeat read only opens the file and checks its digests; measured on the largest saved conversation before and after.

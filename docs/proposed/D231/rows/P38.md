# P38 - The conversation window shows only what has something to show

Row 205 of the 'Advocate build plan' sheet, written out by plan_export.py. The sheet is the record (P7).

## ID

P38

## Item

The conversation window shows only what has something to show

## Description

(a) A new matter shows the conversation alone: no matter board and no reading pane. (b) The matter board appears once it has something on it: when the board reads ready from the conversation's saved understanding (a turn still being worked on included, P26), or when it cannot be read, since a failure to read is said, never hidden as an empty board; an earlier matter record's board likewise once it has disputes or a failure to say. A board waiting for its first content, or being read for the first time, is not shown. On a phone, the Matter board button appears with the board. The Files button stays beside the board's title, so it appears with the board. (c) The reading pane is closed by default at every width and opens only when a link, a board item or Files is opened; Close or Escape closes it on a wide window too. This replaces the 18 Oct design's reading pane always beside the answer on a wide window. The conversation takes the room the closed panes leave. (d) The window stays still: no scroll bar for the whole page; each part (the board, the conversation, the reading pane, the conversation history) scrolls on its own, and its scroll bar shows only while the pointer is over that part or something in it has the focus. (e) The conversation uses the whole width its column has, and so does the answer's text, to the window's full width (P50, 25 Oct 2026, replacing P45's reading width); when the reading pane opens, the conversation narrows to make room for it, at every width but a phone's, where the pane fills the screen (replacing the pane lying over the conversation on a narrower window). A new matter's first message box stays a centred box.

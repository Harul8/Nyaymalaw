DECISIONS (the owner's, in the words above; Claude's wording, for the owner to correct).
D134. STEP BY STEP, STOPPING AT EVERY STEP. A message is worked one step at a time, and NM stops after each step for the advocate's go-ahead or feedback. NM's own questions stop and wait for the answer too: no default is applied after a wait. For a matter the steps are: (1) read the papers sent and understand what is asked; (2) the disputes, put on the matter board for review; (3) for a dispute the advocate chooses, its legal questions; (4) that dispute's research; (5) its written analysis, checked and reviewed as today. Each step after the first runs only on the advocate's go-ahead.
D135. END TO END ONLY WHEN SAID. When the advocate explicitly says to go ahead end to end (in words or with a button), NM does every remaining step without stopping, on its own understanding, and says what it assumed where it would otherwise have asked.
D136. ONLY WHAT IS ASKED. NM does what the advocate asks for, not a standard workflow: the steps it proposes follow the request, and it never runs a step the advocate did not ask for. This supersedes the owner's standing purpose that every matter is researched for where the law stands on each dispute unless the advocate rules it out (AN.28, after the second paid replay of 31 Oct 2026).
D137. A CORRECTION REPLACES NM'S READING EVERYWHERE. When the advocate corrects what NM read from a paper (a date, a survey number, a party), the correction replaces NM's reading in every later step, marked 'as you told us', and only the work that depended on the corrected reading is redone. (Claude's detail, for the owner to correct: NM's original reading stays in the saved record, and a saved answer stays as saved, P46.)
D138. THE PAPERS ARE READ AFTER SEND. Reading the papers for facts starts at Send, not at upload, so that Send is never delayed. A later message reuses what was read and reads only papers not read before.
D139. THE DISPUTES GO ON THE MATTER BOARD. The owner's explicit yes (message 3) to change the board that D60 froze: the disputes NM identifies go on the matter board for the advocate's review. D60 otherwise stands. OPEN: what each dispute shows there (its stage and next step, or the dispute alone).
D140. TENTATIVE TIMES. At each stop NM shows how long its next step should take, labelled tentative, and each estimate is kept beside the time the step actually took. Cost stays internal (AN.37).
D141. THE SPEC, agreed (message 4). SEES: a tentative time after Send and at every stop; at each stop, what was done and what comes next. MUST NEVER: run a step the advocate did not ask for (outside end to end); research without the advocate's go-ahead; use a reading the advocate corrected; show an unchecked answer; lose finished steps while the advocate is away. WHEN IT FAILS: the step says why in plain words and offers Retry; nothing after it starts; the earlier steps stay. PASSING: on a real matter every step stops for the advocate, and 'end to end' runs to the end without stopping; a corrected fact shows corrected everywhere after it, with only its dependents redone (counted in calls); tentative times compared with the actual ones.
D142. WHAT THIS CHANGES IN AN.37. The two-minute wait and default of D39 and D50 are superseded: in interactive mode a question waits for its answer; in end to end NM goes on on its own understanding (D135). The question window itself (D37) stands.

## Item

Response engine

## Description

Answers the advocate's legal question from retrieved passages. Each legal statement is bound to the passage that supports it, every citation is checked by E1, adverse authority is shown, and found / not found / not assessed is stated. Quick questions A1-A3 first.
POC: offline answers on a fixed question set, every statement traced to its passage.
Wired: answers in the existing chat.
Production: grounding gates and the golden conversations at the pass bar.

## Governs or depends on

E1 case and statute identity/readback checks, E2, S3 and P10; 3.4, 3.5, 5.4.1. No statutory claim is released before its reference and source version are checked.

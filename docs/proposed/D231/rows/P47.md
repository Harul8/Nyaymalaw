# P47 - A step counts as taken only when the facts say it was

Row 220 of the 'Advocate build plan' sheet, written out by plan_export.py. The sheet is the record (P7).

## ID

P47

## Item

A step counts as taken only when the facts say it was

## Description

In the time-limit step, 'done' (the step a time limit governs) is given only when the advocate or a document says the step has already been taken, with its date and the words saying so; or, where they say it was taken but not when, the words and no date (P36 (a), unchanged: it then shows as taken, its date not given). Words saying the step has not been taken, or not yet, are not words saying it was: where that is all they say, and where they say nothing about the step, 'done' is left empty, and the limit stands open or passed as of the day it was worked out, with its days. Code's checks of the words (they are in the conversation, and are not the words of the event time runs from) are unchanged: whether words say a step was taken is the model's judgment, so the repair is the step's prompt, with no new contract and no new call.

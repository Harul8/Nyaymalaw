## Description

ENTRY: each legal question, original factual qualifications, complete conversation context and distinct legal enquiries.
DOES: hybrid search per enquiry across held Acts and judgments; rank candidates; read back exact source text with parent provision or surrounding paragraphs.
PRODUCES: owned source IDs, exact text, locations/version/digest, enquiry associations, ranking and explicit coverage/failures; target up to six candidates per corpus per question.
CERTIFIES: source readback and mechanical identity checks only. Relevance, speaker, court treatment, applicability and current validity remain separately reviewed judgments for E3; a digest cannot establish them. Preserve unknowns and retrieve context needed for adoption/rejection.
REFUSES: incompatible source/index lineage and unowned text.
RECOVERS: retain independent successful searches and disclose affected gaps; never label a partial search complete.
EXIT: immutable replayable candidate snapshot with checked and unassessed fields separated.
EVAL: measure source identity, relevance, attribution and recall independently before production; thresholds remain to be specified. Exact statute readback must satisfy P3 before a reference is displayed.

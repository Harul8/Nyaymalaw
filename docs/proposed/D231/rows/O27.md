## Description

The owner, looking at the redesigned app: "UI change in the app -- I don't want the beige color as background color, keep it white/ transparent -- let me know if you think otherwise". CLAUDE'S VIEW, given with the change: agreed. The page, the conversation, the matter board and the sign-in page become white. Three effects the owner may reverse: (1) a white card on a white page is told apart by its thin border and no longer by tone; (2) the few small tinted spots that shared the beige - table header rows, hover highlights, quiet labels - take a barely-there cool grey so that hovering and headers still show; (3) the amber and brass washes that mean a warning or a limit are colours with a meaning, not backgrounds, and stay. Not changed, because the owner said 'the app': the public site's pages, which keep the signed-off ivory (offered).

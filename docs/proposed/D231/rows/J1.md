# J1 - Who is speaking in a judgment

Row 122 of the 'Advocate build plan' sheet, written out by plan_export.py. The sheet is the record (P7).

## ID

J1

## Item

Who is speaking in a judgment

## Description

Every statement in a judgment keeps its original speaker: a party's submission, a lower court, an earlier judgment quoted, or the deciding court. A proposition is attributed to the deciding court only where its own reasoning adopts it, and the scope of that adoption, rejection or qualification is kept; majority and dissent stay apart; never an isolated sentence without its context (stage 16).

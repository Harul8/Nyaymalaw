## Description

THE ASK: "Now let us work on redesigning the website. You can now use Claude design capabilities to do this. Give me your ideas in terms of how you want to design this, assuming we are going to host this as a public website. Okay, so once people open the website, they open nyamala.in. What is that they will see? Okay, how does that homepage look like? Then how do they go about registering if they want to register? And how would the login page look like? And once they log in, how the actual tool look like? And once they log in and start interacting with NM, then I think most of the work which is today should still remain the same, other than making it look good from a design and aesthetics point of view. So first, let me know at a high level how would the design work look like. What is your plan for the design work? And once we are okay with the design plan, second, we will do mock-up creation. And once the mock-ups are finalized, we will go ahead and design the actual tool." (dictated; 'nyamala.in' was the domain, which the owner then gave as nyaymalaw.in). CLAUDE'S SEVEN QUESTIONS, after a plan for the homepage, registration, sign-in, the tool and the way of working: (1) the exact domain; (2) launch by waitlist or invitation, or open registration; (3) the public site separate from the app or not; (4) the visual direction, or both mocked; (5) show plans and prices now or hide them; (6) an optional professional-details step in registration; (7) languages. THE ANSWERS: "1.nyaymalw.in 2.open registration 3. Whatever we develop here will be deployed as public website/tool 4. Think and design what looks good for our purpose, a professional legal tool for advocates 5. Show the plan, put Pro 5000, Elite 10000 and Enterprise custom amounts > 15000 6. Yes 7. English only, no other languages, however tool should be able to understand Telugu later; then, correcting the first: nyaymalaw.in".

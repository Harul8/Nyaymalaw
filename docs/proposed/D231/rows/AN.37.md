D37 (agreed): THE QUESTION WINDOW. When an answer only the advocate can give would change the work, NM asks it in a window over the workspace: one to four questions, each with a short heading, the question, one line on what depends on the answer, and two to four choices (one, or several ticked); always a box for the advocate's own words, and 'Go on without this', which applies the default NM states. The model proposes questions within steps it already runs; code decides whether one is shown and owns the mechanical triggers; a question that changes nothing is never built (the rule of the declared gate G-GAP). The progress line says NM is waiting for an answer. The answer joins the conversation as the advocate's own words, seen by every later step and by the independent review, and is saved with the turn; reopening shows what NM asked and what was answered exactly as saved (P46). What NM shows of usage is a percentage only (O21; item 6).
D38 (agreed): MUST NEVER. Ask what the conversation or the papers already answer; ask the same thing twice in a conversation; show dollars; choose one of the advocate's facts for them in advance; carry a link, or ask for a password, a one-time code or a payment; wait forever; rebuild a saved answer.
D39 (agreed; the owner's own rule): WAITING. Work that does not depend on the answer carries on meanwhile. Work that depends on it waits two minutes; then NM applies the stated default and moves on. The question stays open for ten minutes, and an answer given within them is acted on (how, where it differs from a default already applied: open, see Status).
D40 (agreed): PAUSE IN PLACE. The turn waits inside itself. If the server restarts while it waits, the turn is saved with what it had (P27) and the open question stays answerable, its answer going as the next message.
D41 (agreed): COST IS AN INTERNAL MEASURE, never a question to the advocate. Every model call's cost is estimated before it is sent and kept beside what it cost; a turn's cost is estimated roughly at its start and again once its plan is known; a counter keeps what it has spent against that estimate. The measure is the owner's, against spiralling cost (what NM does when the counter passes the estimate: open, see Status).
D42 (agreed): THE ALLOWANCE BEFORE EVERY CALL. What is left of the advocate's allowance is checked before every model call, not only before a turn starts, and a conversation stops in the middle once it has run out. This amends AN.17 ('work already running finishes and is charged') for calls not yet sent (details: open, see Status).
D43 (agreed; the owner left the order to Claude, item 8): ORDER OF BUILD. 37a: the questions under 'What I need from you' answered in a form below the answer, the answers going as the next message in the advocate's words. 37b: the estimate on every call, the turn's counter and the allowance before every call. 37c: the question channel. 37d: questions at the understanding - whom the advocate acts for, what they want, which disputes to work on. 37e: the rest - a name that matches several Acts or judgments, pages that could not be read, a paper that seems to belong to another client, a fact that changes the law applied. ## Notes

WHAT EXISTS, read in the code 3 Nov 2026. A turn is one long request; the page reads its progress every two seconds, and the progress carries stage names and counts only (nm/app/turn_progress.py); what a turn shows is sealed beside its conversation (nm/core_engine/conversation.py, SHOWN). Every model call of a turn passes one place (nm/core_engine/calls.py, TurnCalls.structured), which already estimates its input before sending and records its cost after. NM asks only at the end of an answer, under 'What I need from you' (response_writer.py), whom the advocate acts for among those questions - after the research and the writing are paid for. The allowance is checked only before a turn starts (nm/app/composition.py, _require_allowance); the trial is 30 cents (nm/arrive/usage_port.py), and on 3 Nov 2026 a one-paper turn measured 10 cents and a two-paper turn 21 cents. Repeats are bounded in code: two rewrites a section, one edit after the check across the sections, 32 corrections a turn, one missing-law round a dispute, five judgments read in full, five sections a question. Not bounded, under D27: the size of a matter, Terra or Sol at Max, and one call writing up to 128,000 tokens; calls are not streamed, so a call's cost is known only when it ends.
MECHANISM (proposed, to be settled piece by piece). Questions: one contract - heading, question, what depends on it, choices, one or several, own words allowed, the default, the sources that show the gap, who asked; code checks each; the question is sealed beside the conversation and the progress says only that one waits; an answer route checks the answer against the choices, seals it and wakes the turn; work that depends on it holds at its next call while the rest goes on; a new turn version and rendering keep earlier answers exactly as saved. Cost: a call's estimate is its input as counted times its model's price, plus the usual output of its step at its effort, read from the usage records and the saved turns (no paid call); kept beside what it cost; the turn's counter is read before each call is sent. Allowance: one check before each paid call, where every call made for an advocate passes (the metered model).

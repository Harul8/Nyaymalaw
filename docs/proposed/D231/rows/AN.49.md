WHAT THE REVIEW FOUND (the review document, section 9 and 9.6; measurements in column 7). One account - what the law requires of a dispute, how the papers stand on each requirement, what to get, what the other side will rely on (case_strength, AN.35) - is drawn in four places: in full inside the dispute's section of the answer, opened by default; as one line for every requirement and every point of the other side on the board; in full again in the reading pane when the dispute's name is pressed; and one requirement at a time when a board line is pressed.

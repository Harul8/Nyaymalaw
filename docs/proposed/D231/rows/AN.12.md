## Description

MUST NEVER: show real client or advocate data; claim what the product cannot evidence; change how the tool behaves or reopen what P39-P45 and P50 decided; use outside fonts, trackers or scripts; show an assumption as if it were decided (each is marked). WHEN IT FAILS: a mock-up the owner does not accept is revised, not built. DONE (stage 2): the owner approves each set, and the approved set and its words are recorded here; the build slices are then written, one concern each. BUILD SLICES (stage 3, not yet agreed): tokens and self-hosted fonts in the stylesheet; sign-in and registration as pages of their own; the tool's shell; the answer's polish; the public pages; plans and usage; accessibility and speed - each checked in the owner's browser, committed and pushed before the next.

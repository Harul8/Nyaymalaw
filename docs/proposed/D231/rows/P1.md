# P1 - Serve advocates only

Row 15 of the 'Advocate build plan' sheet, written out by plan_export.py. The sheet is the record (P7).

## ID

P1

## Item

Serve advocates only

## Description

Nyaymalaw serves advocates, with their juniors and colleagues. A client's account reaches Nyaymalaw as the advocate reports it, and advice is addressed to the advocate, who decides and acts. A plain-language explanation for a client is a draft the advocate sends (C7). Nyaymalaw never acts in the world: it does not file, sign, send, appear, or contact anyone.

## Governs or depends on

Governs 1.2.6, 1.4.4, 5.4.6, 6.1.7 and 6.3.1. Open (suggestion, not decided): how a user is established as an enrolled advocate at sign-up.

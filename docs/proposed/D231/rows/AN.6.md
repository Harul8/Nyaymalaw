## Description

SEES: on the first screen, an answer to what the advocate asked: a row per dispute (what to do, by when, what to have ready) and what to do first; then each dispute in the same order with its detail folded; one checklist of what NM needs; NM's checks folded at the end; plain, short sentences in the advocate's own words; lines of a readable width. MUST NEVER: fold or move away anything the check found misleading; fold a paragraph the advice depends on; drop a condition that changes the advice; give a date code did not work out; show NM's working words or its review notes as part of the answer; change how an answer saved earlier is shown. WHEN IT FAILS: a dispute held back, or a time limit not worked out, says why in its In short row in one plain line, and the rest shows; a section with nothing to fold shows as it is; an answer saved under an earlier version keeps its form. DONE: offline, the new rendering checked and earlier answers replayed unchanged; then a bounded live run on the cheque-and-consignment matter and two others: every part of the question answered on the first screen, no point repeated across the opening, the sections and the checklist, no NM working words, lines within 90 characters, every source and checklist link opening; then the owner's read in the browser.

## Description

Not started. | CONTRACT AFTER O24 (25 Oct 2026). COUNTS: every request NM makes to a paid AI service for an advocate - each model call of a turn (understanding, research, the time limits, writing, the review, rewrites, the check across the sections, the overview), the reading of page images, the transcription of voice notes and dictation, and live dictation - whatever its outcome. CHARGED: the cost the provider's own usage report establishes, at the versioned prices in nm/shared/model_config.py (cached input, cache writes and long-context rates included; audio by the minute); a call that fails after the provider answered is charged at the usage it reports; a call the provider did not answer, or whose usage it did not report, is counted and kept with its error and charged nothing, its cost recorded as not established. KEPT: per account and content-free (no prompt, message or answer): when, what operation, which model, the outcome, the attempts, the tokens or audio seconds, and the cost, in one sidecar store beside the turn attempts, the account named by a digest. TRIAL: USD 0.30 of usage, given once to each account the first time its allowance is read (the first sign-in for a new account, the next sign-in for an existing one) and never again, the store holding one trial per account. An operator can add usage to an account from the operator host (no web route): how the owner keeps developing past the trial until payment exists. REFUSED: once nothing is left, work that would call the paid AI service is refused before it starts - a new message, the reading of page images, a voice note, dictation, live dictation - in the owner's words of P51, code usage_limit, nothing saved and the message kept in the box; work already running finishes and is charged, so an allowance can end a little below nothing, shown as nothing left; reopening a saved answer makes no call and is never refused. SHOWN: the percentage of the allowance left (O21), whole numbers, never cents or rupees: the top-bar chip and the notices of P53, the step each notice reached kept with the account so each shows once wherever the advocate signs in. PAYMENT: later, by UPI (owner); plan credits come with it. PIECES: the store's port; the store; the metered model wrapper; its wiring on both model routes, with the gate; the routes (usage, notices, and the refusals of dictation and page reading); the operator command; then the chip and the notices (index.html, app.js, app.css), each with focused tests.

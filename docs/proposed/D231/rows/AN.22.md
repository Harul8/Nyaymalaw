## Item

Build: a Home button in the app's top bar, and the signed-in person on the website's top bar (P52, O28)

## Description

SEES, IN THE APP: a Home link (a house and the word Home; the house alone on a phone) at the left of the frozen top bar, before + New matter. It opens the website's home page, '/', in the same tab; unsent writing is kept on the device and an answer being prepared carries on in the server, as when the page is reloaded. SEES, ON THE WEBSITE: the top bar stays at the top as the page scrolls (a white strip with a thin line beneath). A visitor not signed in sees Sign in and Create account as today. A signed-in visitor sees in their place the person control of the app - the initials circle, the name (not on a phone) and a menu holding the name, the email, Open the app and Sign out - and every Create account / Create your account / Choose-a-plan link in the page reads 'Open your workspace' and opens the app, the page's own Sign in button being hidden. THE MECHANISM: one small script of ours, shared by the seven public pages, asks the server only 'who is signed in?' (GET /api/session, a read that does not extend the session) and, for Sign out, calls the existing sign-out route; the initials rule lives in one shared file used by the app and the website. The bar draws the profile at once when the browser carries the readable sign-in marker (nm_csrf), so there is no flicker, and falls back to the buttons if the answer is not 'signed in'. MUST NEVER: show a profile to someone not signed in; read a matter or anything but who is signed in from a public page; call any route but the two; say someone is signed out unless the server confirmed it; treat a sign-out the app left unconfirmed (nm_retirement) as signed in; leave a visitor without the buttons if the script cannot run; change anything else in the app. WHEN IT FAILS: a failed or negative answer leaves the buttons; a sign-out that cannot be confirmed says so in the menu and keeps the profile. AMENDS AN.16: 'a public page runs no script' becomes 'a public page runs only this script, which asks only this' (tests/test_the_public_site_stays_static_and_local.py). PIECES (one production file each, with focused tests): shared initials file; the app's Home link (index.html, app.css) with the F-A-17 contract; the website script; its style and the frozen strip (site.css); the seven pages; the declarations and the guard. PASS: the offline tests pass and the owner sees it in their browser. FAILURE SCOPE: display. RECOVERY: revert the commits.

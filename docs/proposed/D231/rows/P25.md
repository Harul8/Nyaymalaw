## ID

## Item

No answer is ever wholly withheld

## Description

A review finding is the model's problem to fix, never the advocate's. Each dispute's section gets up to three tries: the draft, and up to two rewrites, each told plainly what the review found and asked to state only what the advocate's facts, the documents and the held sources support, saying what is missing rather than asserting it. After the third review, the statements still found wrong are dropped, statement by statement (not whole sections), and the rest is reviewed once more as trimmed, because dropping a qualifier can change what remains; a misquote or misattribution is never released. Where nothing of a section survives, the advocate gets a short note built by code alone, clearly labelled as not a full answer: the facts as given, the provisions found with their exact words, and the questions still open. The advocate is always told what was left out and why. Supersedes Q15 and the 18 Oct one-rewrite setting.

## Notes

CLAUDE'S VIEW, 3 Nov 2026 (proposed, not agreed). Auto as a fourth choice beside 6 Luna, 6.1 Terra and 6.1 Sol; Luna stays the default until a measured comparison shows Auto better at a cost the owner accepts. Under Auto, which model a step runs on is code's table, like the effort table, set by measurement. A piece of work moves to a stronger model on evidence code can see rather than on a forecast: a section the independent review has faulted twice (its last try), an understanding its check still faults after revising, papers that differ on a fact a time limit rests on. Where Luna already judges, its own marks (a question it finds unsettled) may route that work. Not Luna deciding call by call: a model rarely sees its own blind spots; it would add a duty or a call to every step; cost would be harder to foresee; and a change of model in the middle of a piece loses its cached input. The stronger model is kept to the few checks with small inputs and high stakes - the understanding's last check (about 8 cents a turn on Terra), the check across the sections, the overview's review - not every review: a review reads 100,000-200,000 tokens, about 15 cents on Terra against 1.5 on Luna. Sol stays out of Auto until measured on the current build. Evidence so far (1 Nov 2026): no step has yet shown Terra worth its cost; Terra and Luna each found problems the other missed.

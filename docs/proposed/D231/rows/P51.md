# P51 - An exhausted AI account is named, never retried, kept in the record and told in the owner's words

Row 230 of the 'Advocate build plan' sheet, written out by plan_export.py. The sheet is the record (P7).

## ID

P51

## Item

An exhausted AI account is named, never retried, kept in the record and told in the owner's words

## Description

TRIGGER: the provider answers a model call with its own 'no credit left' error (OpenAI: type insufficient_quota, code credit_balance_exhausted; seen 25 Oct 2026). NAMED: the transport (nm/shared/model_transport.py) raises it as UsageLimitReached, a model error of its own and not a rate limit; it is never retried, since a retry cannot succeed. KEPT: the call's record names it (UsageLimitReached) and a refused turn's diagnosis carries it, with no private data and not the provider's message. TOLD, in the owner's words, defined once (nm/shared/model_port.py): 'You have run out of your usage limits. Please upgrade to continue accessing NM.' - wherever NM now says 'the AI service did not respond' for this failure: (a) a turn that cannot be answered is refused with those words (code usage_limit, nothing saved, the same message can be sent again after upgrading); (b) a section, the check across the sections or the overview that could not be made is saved with the reason 'usage' and its notice says the same in the same place, so a saved answer is never taken for complete; (c) a turn all of whose sections fail so is refused whole and a turn some of whose sections were made is saved with the notices, exactly as for any service failure; saved records with the reason reopen as saved (P46). NOT CHANGED: rate limits keep their bounded retry; every other provider failure; the dollar cap of an evaluation; no prompt, contract version or model call changes. DEPENDENT WORK, recorded not built: dictation, live dictation and the reading of page images still say 'just now' and 'try again' for this failure. IN PRODUCTION these words would tell an advocate to upgrade when the cause is NM's own account credit; right for development, to revisit when plans exist.

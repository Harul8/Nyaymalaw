## The unit of delivery

Choose one observable user outcome and its smallest complete route through
interface, domain, persistence and proof. The Start record names the relevant
PRD features, step contracts, professional standards, existing mechanisms and
atomic criteria. A foundation can close independently; an integration row
owns the later combined outcome. Dependencies cannot point to a foundation
whose own acceptance still requires its consumer.

An intended contract is not evidence of implementation. Existing code is not
evidence of a successful served journey. A served demonstration is not evidence
of professional quality across representative matters. Preserve those three
distinctions as each item progresses.

## The working loop

Retrieve authorised material already held, listen to what the advocate adds,
reflect the understood task, form provisional hypotheses and identify the
smallest gap that can change urgency, characterization, proof, remedy or the
next decision. Ask for that fact or material and reassess readiness. Accept
typed, spoken, uploaded and mixed inputs through the declared capability and
privacy boundary; do not force the advocate to retype available material.

Stop when the task has a useful answer at a permitted maturity, when required
material is unavailable, when authority is missing, when a justified refusal
applies, or when the advocate pauses. Name the consequence, owner and resume
trigger. Do not continue questioning merely because a field is empty.

New material can reopen briefing or legal work. Preserve prior versions and
unaffected conclusions; invalidate only dependent positions and permissions.
A prior considered answer can become provisional. A strong recommendation
does not become authority to send, concede, settle or file.

The bounded-autonomy amendment lets the lead choose and revise permitted
investigations and selectively delegate research or draft preparation. It does
not turn packet prerequisites into a fixed interview or let an agent change
permissions, budgets, source status or publication gates. BK-90 records the
plan change; BK-91/BK-92 retain the future runtime proof. P47 supplies delegated
task foundations before P46's lead integration; P24/P29/P35/P37 supply their
remaining interaction, artifact, professional and economic acceptance.

## What completion means

The wave definitions and release profiles in `backlog/plan.json` separate:

- **Feature conformance:** the promised behavior exists and meets its contract.
- **Served-journey conformance:** the enabled route succeeds, refuses, recovers
  and returns correctly in the actual application, with current full-population proof.
- **Professional conformance:** qualified review of representative tasks finds
  the advice useful, candid, supported and appropriate to the instruction.
- **Deployment approval:** the named environment, users, data, providers,
  operations and enabled scope meet their approved release conditions.

Prototype, bounded pilot and production are distinct release profiles, not
synonyms for these progress states. No profile grants approval by itself.
Neither local Phase A completion nor D-013 grants a production MFA exception.

P01 now enforces the local specification/checked-tree boundary: current feature
state comes from the registry, historical verdicts remain history, applicable
promises and delivery relations move the identity, and exact declared failures
can support only a scoped build stamp. BK-80 remains partial because P03 still
owns release-profile semantics, qualified reviewer and configuration identity,
complete browser/model/counsel/production populations and verified approval
authority. Until those land, sign-off explicitly reviews the limits and cannot
claim that a local green or scoped count proves them automatically.

## Professional review that can change the decision

Use the existing PA/EW/AM standards and predeclare the task population, rubric,
reviewer qualifications and critical failures. Review practical utility,
controlling issues, primary support, strongest adverse case, remedy value,
question economy, uncertainty and the next action. An invented authority or
unsupported decisive conclusion cannot disappear into an average score.

Validate the legal premises separately from deterministic computations.
Correct arithmetic does not prove the accrual rule, applicable law or forum
was correct; a corpus cohort count does not establish binding authority.
Named primary-source and qualified review must support those propositions.
Changed facts, law, model, corpus or instructions reopen affected approval.

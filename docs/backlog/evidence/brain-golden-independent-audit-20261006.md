Audit decision: acceptable for the declared offline mechanical wiring scope. This is an audit of exact repository scenario source provenance, authored fixture labels, raw fabricated provider responses, and the real admission/recovery/save/replay/release boundaries. It does not qualify real-model semantics, newly acquired court text, or all expectations of the original multi-turn golden conversations.

Audited artifacts and final hashes are pinned in `/tmp/nm-golden-corpus/pressure-audit-guard.json`.

## Source and selection

- GS-06 through GS-25 are the twenty distinct matter-bearing curated scenarios. Eight fixed groups partition them exactly once, four groups of three and four of two.
- The source packet retains every exact original scenario row, 40 selected exact quote spans, original offsets/line numbers and a fixed source hash. All 40 selected quotations reach the combined rehearsal input unchanged.
- Scenario descriptions, sample advocate messages, opponent positions, work requests and legal-rubric prose remain distinguishable. Pure legal predictions such as injunction failure are not silently adopted as client facts.
- Downloaded judgment PDFs are unavailable: all 20 selected public-object fetches were blocked by proxy 403. No PDF hash verification, verbatim court findings, or authentic merged-client representation is claimed.
- Independent source test rerun: five passed. Direct source-partition and oracle guards passed across all eight groups.

## Fabrication and owner boundaries

- Provider output is logged before strict `require_schema`, with complete raw object and operation input. Complete schema-rejected output goes through the production SchemaViolation/quarantine boundary; the fixture does not repair it after rejection.
- The semantic support map is closed. Each accepted/rejected proposition and source is predeclared; unfamiliar meanings raise fixture-definition assertion. Kind, basis, placement, matter scope and dispute assignment declarations are checked as well.
- Shared canned after-the-fact verdict/source/coverage fillers are not used. Dynamic selection of code-owned IDs resolves predeclared source/issue labels and does not fabricate new facts.
- Seventy-two public composite cases include 64 quality/failure variants plus eight issue/assignment cases. The latter declare independent issues, reject invented disputes, inject a foreign assignment sibling and preserve independently valid material.
- Reopened material assignment IDs are resolved through reopened dispute labels and compared to independently authored assignment labels, so transport and source/thread correctness have separate observations.
- Public cases invoke POST /api/turn, reload FileMatterStore, inspect records and operation receipts, and compare saved exact replies to release.
- The specialist boundary tests additionally cover precise legal/record references, empty versus contradictory metadata, code-owned acknowledgements, absent effects, other owned targets, real two-turn GS-15 dated correction, wholly unread required readers, failed commits and lost acknowledgements/replay.

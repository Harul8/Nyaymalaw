## LB-194: constrained fresh response expressions

Invariant: a model block label, source ID or positive review cannot authorize
arbitrary operational or factual prose. An accurate code status does not cure
a false assertion elsewhere in the reply.

Fresh responses require versioned constrained expressions across **every block
kind**, including accounts, reasoning, questions, follow-ups and recovery. Code
resolves owned original evidence and confirmed execution outcomes. Status and
effect expressions are rendered from checked effects; source expressions use
the original selected words, attribution and context. Do not leave an arbitrary
text escape hatch inside a supposedly safe expression.

Change schemas, model-facing construction and final rendering in staged owning
slices. Each slice changes at most one production file and one prompt, with
focused tests and documentation. An inseparable cross-file contract migration
must be identified before expanding a slice. Preserve independently requested
work; do not replace useful legal work with an internal rejection explanation.

Observable pass condition: both public false-prose fixtures remain prevented
even when a reviewer is deliberately wrong. Their legitimate neighbours deliver
useful supported content, persisted records match the reply, and exact replay
does not repeat effects or model calls.

Durable replay is explicitly versioned. Fresh output follows the new contract;
historical accepted messages retain their original rendering version and exact
saved reply. Do not silently reinterpret old data as newly validated evidence,
or rewrite the durable history to conceal an earlier false reply.

## LB-195: complete propositions and source portions

Invariant: matching words are not proof of semantic support. A date may occur
inside a denial, hypothesis or opponent account. Judge the actor, event,
attribution, negation, chronology, uncertainty and target relationship together.

Use original evidence first in the existing material/dispute review calls;
upstream classifier conclusions must not anchor the independent judgment.
Keep those classifications in code-owned dependencies and admission checks.
For mixed passages, select exact owned substantive portions while retaining the
whole original passage and the qualifiers needed to understand it. Allow
overlapping portions when their context is shared. Code validates identity,
exact text, bounds and ownership; the model judges relevance and entailment.

Observable pass condition: wrong support or invented portions cannot pass their
mechanical admission boundary. Faithful paraphrases, attributed allegations,
uncertain accounts and multiple dates/events retain their correct meaning and
avoid unnecessary rejection. Changes in selected portions invalidate dependent
reviews and survive saving/replay through their explicit versioned contracts.

Exact quoting guarantees fidelity of the displayed words, not that the speaker
adopted them as fact, that a selected portion entails a paraphrase, or that a
legal conclusion applies. Keep these semantic limits explicit.

## LB-196: coverage and targeted recovery

Invariant: an empty envelope does not certify examination, and a rejected
candidate cannot count as represented account. New records are not required
when current records already faithfully represent the original account.

Extend the existing coverage review, within its current call, with source-linked
material proposition dispositions: represented by admitted/current evidence,
missing, unresolved, outside authorized scope or non-account. Do not impose a
sentence-to-record quota or ask a second routine model merely to restate coverage.

Coverage-backed source-purpose disagreements may reach the existing
candidate-free reconsideration even when extraction produced no candidate.
Use one turn-owned recovery ledger, precise failed-unit feedback and the full
original context. A changed role or substantive portion invalidates dependent
decisions. Preserve valid peers; history, identity, ownership and commit-integrity
failures retain their established whole-turn stops.

Observable pass condition: omissions with no candidate trigger bounded recovery
or remain truthfully unfinished; complete empty extraction and already-current
records remain useful successes. Exhausted recovery does not fabricate completion
or make the advocate repeat accepted input.

## LB-197: paired end-to-end qualification

For each unsafe regression, include legitimate neighbours: faithful paraphrases,
source attribution, negation, multiple dates and actors, uncertainty, mixed
instructions/accounts, historical repair without fresh factual assertion,
already-correct records, valid empty extraction and useful independent units.

Inspect the public service outcome, exact delivered expressions, admitted and
persisted records, partial preservation, failed saves and durable replay. Keep
deliberately incorrect accepting reviewers: they establish which code controls
survive semantic failure. Do not declare passing characterizations prevented.

A new rejection must identify a consequential mismatch and affect only
dependent work where the gate contract permits. Normalize demonstrably harmless
metadata; do not repair factual ambiguity silently. Gate counts show friction,
not a false-positive rate. Record false acceptance and false rejection separately
on independently labelled cases, along with useful delivery and terminal failure.

## Calls, checkpoints and evidence

No routine model stage is planned. Strengthen existing writer, verifier,
classifier, coverage and candidate-free recovery owners. Conditional repair is
triggered only by typed consequential failures and uses the existing turn bound.
Measure logical calls and provider attempts separately through the public
service. The earlier common material-review fixture has an eight-call baseline;
exact replay has zero. Those are fixture baselines, not a universal call promise.

At each checkpoint record the owning invariant, affected file/prompt, meaningful
pass condition, exact check command/evidence, routine and conditional call impact,
remaining limits and tested revision in its Before Build row. Commit and push a
coherent verified slice before moving to the next. Apply the new helper with a
JSON owner update rather than editing unrelated workbook cells.

The reusable helper is
`development_environment/one_off_tools/brain_semantic_build_record.py`. It checks
saved-byte preservation of unrelated cells, styles, comments, links, native sheet
controls, workbook controls, row dimensions, owner identities and executable
feature population. Pre-existing mirror mismatches are recorded; no new
unrelated mismatch may be introduced. Oversized cell updates fail instead of
silently truncating history.

The offline phase can establish exercised contract guarantees. Real-model
semantic accuracy, natural-language usefulness, live token cost/latency and
browser-visible acceptance remain **not verified** until the deferred evaluation.
